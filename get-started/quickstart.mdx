title: "Quickstart"
description: "Create an account, give Marky a task, run a tool, and find your results."
---

This walkthrough takes about five minutes. You'll ask Marky Agent for something, run one of the tools, and then find both results in your history.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to [easy-peasy.ai](https://easy-peasy.ai/login) and sign up with Google, Facebook, Apple, or your email address. If you use email, open the confirmation link we send you.

    New accounts start on the Free plan, so you can try everything on this page before choosing a plan. See [Plans and credits](/account/plans-and-credits) for what's included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Give Marky a task">
    The Home screen opens with one question: **How can I help you today?** Type what you need in the box and press **Enter**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/get-started/quickstart-prompt.webp" alt="The Home prompt box with a request typed in: write a welcome email for a yoga studio" />
    </Frame>

    Marky opens a chat and writes the answer. Keep the conversation going to change the result: "make it shorter", "add a line about parking", "translate it to Spanish".

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/get-started/quickstart-marky-reply.webp" alt="Marky's reply in the chat: a welcome email for Sunrise Yoga with a subject line, greeting, and sign-off" />
    </Frame>

    <Tip>
      The buttons under the prompt box start common jobs in one click: **Create slides**, **Build website**, **Images**, **Music**, **Video**, and **More**.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run a tool">
    Tools are single-purpose generators with a short form to fill in. Click **All Tools** in the sidebar, then search or pick a category.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/get-started/quickstart-tools.webp" alt="The Tools page with a search box, category filters such as Writing, Social Media, and Marketing, and tool cards" />
    </Frame>

    Open a tool, fill in the fields, and click **Generate**. This example uses **Instagram Post Caption**. Each result has buttons to **Edit**, **Favorite**, **Share**, **Save**, and **Copy** it. See [Run a tool](/tools/run).

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/get-started/quickstart-tool-output.webp" alt="A generated Instagram caption under the Generate button, with Edit, Favorite, Share, Save, and Copy buttons" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your results">
    Everything you create is saved. Click **History** in the sidebar to see it, sorted into tabs such as **Text**, **Images**, **Videos**, and **Music**. Marky conversations appear here too; click **Open Chat** to continue one.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/get-started/quickstart-history.webp" alt="The History page listing the Instagram caption and the Marky Agent email" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
