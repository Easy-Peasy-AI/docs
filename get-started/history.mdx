**History** in the sidebar keeps what you create, sorted by type and newest first.

</Frame>

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Text** | Results from [tools](/tools/overview) and Marky's replies, 50 per page. **Open Chat** on a reply goes back to its conversation. Search finds items by their text |
| **Favorites** | Results you marked with **Favorite** |
| **Images**, **Videos** | Everything from [AI Images](/ai-images/overview) and [AI Videos](/videos/overview) |
| **AI Text-to-Speech**, **AI Audio Generator**, **Sound Effects**, **Music** | Your [audio](/audio/text-to-speech) |
| **Trash** | What you deleted, on two tabs: **Text**, and **Images & videos** |

Each item has the same buttons as where you made it: copy, save, share, and so on. On a Teams plan, the account owner can filter **Images** and **Videos** by team member.

## Restore or empty the trash

Deleted text results, images, and videos go to **Trash**.

- To bring back a text result, click its restore icon.
- On the **Images & videos** tab, select items and click **Restore**. **Delete forever** deletes just the selected items.

**Empty Trash** permanently deletes everything on the tab you're on. For images and videos, the files are deleted too, so their links stop working everywhere, including where you shared them or used them in a Marky chat. It can't be undone. The words and credits those items used still count in your usage for the period.

<Note>
  Songs, sound effects, and speech don't go to the trash. Deleting them is immediate and can't be undone. Deleting anything doesn't give back the credits or characters it used.
</Note>

**Favorites** and the **Text** tab of **Trash** show your 200 most recent items. The **Images & videos** tab shows all of them, 48 per page.
