**Analytics** shows how much your agent is used and how visitors feel about its answers. Open your agent and click **Analytics**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/agents/analytics.webp" alt="Agent Analytics for the last 14 days: Total Chats 2, Total Messages 6, Positive Feedback 0, Negative Feedback 0, and a Daily Activity chart of messages and chats" />
</Frame>

Choose a period at the top: the last 7, 14, 30, or 90 days, or **Custom** for any range up to 120 days.

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Chats**, **Total Messages** | How many conversations and messages there were |
| **Positive Feedback**, **Negative Feedback** | Thumbs up and down visitors gave the agent's answers. Visitors can rate answers when **Collect User Feedback** is on in [Appearance](/agents/appearance#chat-features) |
| **Daily Activity** | Messages, chats, likes, and dislikes per day |
| **Sentiment Distribution** | How many conversations were positive, negative, or neutral |
| **Sentiment Trend Over Time** | How sentiment changed from day to day |

## Use it to improve your agent

- **Negative feedback or negative sentiment** points to answers worth checking. Open the conversation in [**Conversations**](/agents/conversations), and click **Revise answer** to fix it.
- **A jump in messages** after a change, such as a new page on your website, shows whether people find the chat.
