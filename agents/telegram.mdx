title: "Telegram"

---

Your agent can answer on Telegram through a bot account you create with Telegram's @BotFather. People find your bot by its username and chat with it like with anyone else. Telegram is available on every plan, including Free.

## Connect Telegram

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Telegram bot">
    In Telegram, open a chat with **@BotFather** and send `/newbot`. Choose a display name, such as "Example Yoga Studio", and a username that ends in `bot`, such as `ExampleYogaBot`. BotFather replies with a token like `1234567890:AAF...`. Copy it and keep it private: anyone with the token controls your bot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the token">
    In Easy-Peasy.AI, open your agent, click **Channels**, and click **Connect** on **Telegram**. Paste the token into **Bot token** and click **Save**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/agents/channel-telegram.webp" alt="The Telegram Integration window with a Bot token field, a Save button, and the Enable integration switch" width="520" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Say hello">
    In Telegram, search for your bot's username, send `/start`, and ask a question.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each token can be connected to one agent. To put another agent on Telegram, create another bot with BotFather.

## How it works

- The bot answers in private chats. Each person's conversation keeps its history, so follow-up questions work.
- People can send photos and screenshots when **Allow image attachments** is on in [Appearance](/agents/appearance#image-attachments). Other files aren't read.
- Your [actions](/agents/actions) work on Telegram.
- Conversations appear in [**Conversations**](/agents/conversations) under **Telegram**.

The bot understands three commands:

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/start` | Starts the conversation with a greeting |
| `/clear` | Clears the conversation history |
| `/ping` | Checks the bot is running; it answers "pong" |

## Make your bot look right

In @BotFather, `/setuserpic` sets the bot's profile photo and `/setdescription` sets the text people see before they tap **Start**. Share your bot's link, `t.me/YourBotUsername`, on your website and social profiles.

## Pause or change the token

To stop replies, open the Telegram card and switch off **Enable integration**. If your token leaks, send `/revoke` to @BotFather and connect the new token.
