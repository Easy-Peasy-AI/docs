## Public access

**Is agent publicly available?** is on for new agents. Leave it on: the chat bubble, the shareable link, and your other website chats need it. With it off, the agent currently answers only in the Playground. **Open in new tab** opens your agent's shareable chat page.

## Answer only from knowledge

**Should the agent use only knowledge when responding?** is on for new agents.

- **On:** the agent searches its [knowledge](/agents/knowledge) for every message and answers from it. When the answer isn't there, it says it doesn't know instead of guessing, and it declines questions unrelated to your content.
- **Off:** the agent doesn't search its knowledge at all. It answers from its instructions and the model's general knowledge, like a general assistant.

Keep it on for customer support. Turn it off only for an agent that shouldn't use documents, such as a general writing helper.

## Share with your team

On a Teams plan, **Share with Team Members** lets everyone on your team view and use the agent. Only the agent's owner can change this. On other plans, **Upgrade to Teams** is shown instead.

## Allowed domains

**Allowed Domains** limits which websites can show your agent's chat. Enter one domain per line, without commas:

```text
example.com
*.example.com
localhost:3000
```

`*.example.com` covers every subdomain. Leave the box empty to allow every website.

<Warning>
  With a list set, the agent answers only chats that come from those websites. The [shareable link](/agents/website#shareable-link) stops working, and so does **Preview**, unless you also add `easy-peasy.ai`. Allowed domains keep your widget off other people's websites; they aren't a way to protect private information.
</Warning>

## Publish to Marky Agent

**Publish to Marky Agent** lets other Easy-Peasy.AI users find your agent in [Marky](/marky/overview) and chat with it. Your agent's name, description, and avatar become visible to them, and they use their own allowance for those conversations. Add an **Author name** to show who made it.

## Delete an agent

Under **Danger Zone**, click **Delete Agent**, type `delete`, and confirm. The agent, its knowledge, and its conversations are deleted and can't be restored. Remove its code from your website and disconnect its channels first, so visitors don't see a broken chat.
