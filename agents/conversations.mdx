**Conversations** shows every chat your agent has had, so you can see what people ask and how the agent answers. It's the fastest way to improve an agent: find a weak answer, correct it, and the agent gets it right next time.

## Read conversations

Open your agent and click **Activity** > **Conversations**.

- The menu at the top chooses the channel: **Web**, **WhatsApp**, **Messenger**, **LINE**, or **Telegram**. **Web** includes the website chat, the shareable link, the Playground, and voice calls.
- Each conversation in the list shows the visitor's country, device, and browser, the time, the first message, and its sentiment once it's been analyzed. A phone icon marks conversations with a voice call, with the number of calls when there was more than one.
- Click a conversation to read it. Scroll down and click **Load more conversations** for older ones.
- The refresh icon beside **Conversations** loads new messages without reloading the page.

## Filter conversations

On the **Web** channel, click the filter icon beside **Conversations** to narrow the list. The filters apply as you pick them, appear as chips under the channel menu, and can be removed one at a time or with **Clear all**.

| Filter | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Started** | Conversations started in the last 24 hours, 7, 30, or 90 days |
| **Sentiment** | Positive, neutral, or negative conversations |
| **Feedback** | Conversations where the visitor gave an answer a thumbs up or a thumbs down |
| **Conversations with a voice call** | Conversations where the visitor called your agent |
| **Visitors who shared contact details** | Conversations where you know the visitor's name, email, or phone number |

Under each answer, a percentage shows how confident the agent was. Low scores point to questions its knowledge doesn't cover well.

<Note>
  Conversations on Slack, email, phone calls, Basecamp, and the REST API aren't listed here.
</Note>

## Voice calls

When a visitor calls your agent from the chat widget, the call is part of the same conversation as their typed messages. To list only conversations with a call, turn on **Conversations with a voice call** in the filters.

In the conversation, a marker shows where each call starts, with its time and length, and another where it ends and why: the caller left, the call ended after silence, it reached the time limit, or an error ended it. A call where nobody spoke gets a single marker.

Each message of a call carries a tag:

| Tag | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Spoken** | The visitor said it, or the agent said its reply out loud |
| **Typed during the call** | The visitor typed it into the call instead of speaking |
| **Spoken and typed** | The visitor's question combined speech and typed text |
| **Interrupted** | The visitor spoke before the agent finished this reply |
| **Voice call** | The message was part of a call made before October 6, 2026, before spoken and typed messages were told apart |

**Details** lists every call with its date, length, number of exchanges, and how it ended, plus the total talk time.

## Conversation details

Click **Details** above the conversation to see who the visitor was and where they came from.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/agents/conversation-details.webp" alt="The Details tab: Contact Anonymous, Source Playground, Status Ongoing, Sentiment positive, Messages 4, Country Singapore, Language en-US, Timezone Asia/Singapore, Browser macOS - Chrome, an IP address, Created, Last activity, and Conversation ID" width="600" />
</Frame>

| Detail | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Contact** | The visitor's name or email when they filled in a lead form or your website passed it on, or **Anonymous** |
| **Source** | Website, Playground, or voice call: how the conversation started |
| **Status** | **Ongoing** while the chat is active, then ended |
| **Sentiment**, **Messages** | The mood of the conversation and how many messages it has |
| **Country**, **Language**, **Timezone**, **Browser**, **IP address** | Where the visitor was and what they used |
| **Started on**, **Last page**, **Referrer** | The page the chat started on, the page they were last on, and where they came from |
| **Created**, **Last activity**, **Conversation ID** | When it started, when the last message was sent, and its ID |

To name visitors who are signed in to your website, pass their details to the chat. See [Chat widget for developers](/developers/chat-widget#identify-the-visitor).

## Revise an answer

When the agent gets something wrong, click **Revise answer** under the answer. Write the answer it should have given in **Expected Response** and click **Save**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/agents/revise-answer.webp" alt="The Revise Answer window with the User Message What classes do you offer?, the agent's response, and an Expected Response to edit" width="560" />
</Frame>

The question and your answer are added to [**Knowledge**](/agents/knowledge) as a Q&A, and the answer is marked **Revised**. The agent uses your answer from the next question on.

## Export and delete

- To download the conversations, click the **⋯** menu beside **Conversations** and choose **Export as PDF** or **Export as JSON**. The export contains every conversation of the channel, not only the filtered ones. It isn't available for Telegram.
- To delete a conversation, hover over it in the list and click the trash icon. Deleting can't be undone.
