**Knowledge** is everything your agent knows about your business. When someone asks a question, the agent looks up the most relevant parts of its knowledge and answers from them. The better and more current this content is, the better the answers.

## Add a source

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Add source">
    Open your agent, click **Knowledge**, and click **Add source**. An agent with no knowledge yet shows the panel straight away.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/agents/knowledge-add-source.webp" alt="Select a data source: Files, Website Crawler, Text, Q&A, Sitemap XML, and Youtube" width="520" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a tab and fill it in">
    | Tab | What to enter |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Website** | Your website's address, then **Find pages**. You see the pages we found and uncheck any your agent shouldn't read. Up to 100 pages are read per website, and pages the agent already knows are skipped. **Add a single page** reads just one page; **Import from a sitemap** reads the pages your sitemap lists |
    | **Files** | PDF, DOCX, CSV, TXT, or EPUB files, up to 10 at a time. Drop them on the panel or browse |
    | **Text** | Any text you type or paste |
    | **Q&A** | A question and the answer you want the agent to give |
    | **YouTube** | A video's link and the language of its transcript. The agent learns from the transcript |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it">
    The source appears at the top of the list as **Queued**, then **Training**, and **Trained** when the agent can use it. While a website is being read, **Training in progress** shows how many of its pages have arrived. The page refreshes on its own.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sources and their status

The summary above the list shows how many sources the agent has and how many are trained, failed, or still training. Click one of those counts to list just those sources.

Each row shows the source's name (for a website page, its path and page title), how many characters it adds, and its status. Filter by type with the chips above the list, by status with **All statuses**, or search.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Queued** | Waiting to be read |
| **Training** | Being read |
| **Trained** | Ready: the agent answers from it |
| **Failed** | Couldn't be read. The reason is shown under the source, for example a page that no longer exists or a site that blocks automated reading |
| **Stuck** | Queued or being read for more than two hours. Use **Try again** |

- The **⋯** menu on each row opens the page, file, or video, shows the text of a Text or Q&A source, reads the source again (**Read again**, or **Try again** after a failure), and deletes it.
- Select several rows with their checkboxes to delete them together. Shift-click selects a range.
- When sources wait in the queue for a while, **Training in progress** offers **Retry them**.

## Training report

After a website is read, a report appears above the list when something needs your attention:

- Pages that couldn't be read, grouped by reason. **Show** lists them, and **Retry failed pages** reads them again, one at a time, when a later read can help. Pages that no longer exist can simply be deleted.
- Pages with almost no text, such as forms and galleries.
- Starter questions the agent can't answer from what it learned. **Add answer** opens a new Q&A with the question filled in.

When nothing needs attention, the report shrinks to a short **Training finished** line for a few hours.

## Keep your knowledge current

Your agent doesn't notice when your website changes. To update it:

- **Retrain** reads all your website pages again and re-learns the ones whose text changed. The agent keeps answering while it runs. Files, text, Q&A, and videos stay as they are; to replace a document, delete it and upload the new version.
- **Auto-retrain**, the switch in the summary, does the same every week. It's available on Unlimited 500 and higher plans.

**Last trained** in the summary shows when the agent last learned something new.

## Fix a wrong answer

When the agent gets something wrong in a real conversation, open it in [**Conversations**](/agents/conversations#revise-an-answer) and click **Revise answer**. Your corrected answer is added to **Knowledge** as a Q&A, so the agent gives it next time. You can also add a **Q&A** here directly.

## Limits

**Characters** in the summary shows how much of your plan's knowledge allowance this agent uses. Each agent has its own allowance, from 1 million characters on the Free plan up to 250 million. On the Free plan, an agent can also have up to 250 sources. See [Limits and usage](/agents/limits-and-usage).

## How the agent uses its knowledge

Your content is split into short passages. For each message, the agent searches for the passages closest to the question and the conversation so far, and answers from them. That means:

- **Clear, specific pages work best.** A page that answers "How much is a class pass?" in one sentence beats a long page that mentions prices in passing.
- **Q&A pairs give exact answers.** Use them for the questions you get most, and for anything the agent keeps getting wrong.
- **Conflicting content causes conflicting answers.** Remove outdated pages and documents.

By default, the agent answers only from its knowledge and says when it doesn't know. You can change this in [**Settings**](/agents/settings#answer-only-from-knowledge).

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A source failed">
    The reason is shown under the source. Click **⋯** and **Try again**. If a web page keeps failing, the site may block automated reading: copy the page's text into a **Text** source, or upload it as a PDF. A YouTube video fails when it has no transcript in the language you chose.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The agent doesn't know something that's in a document">
    Make sure the source is **Trained**. Scanned PDFs without selectable text can't be read; upload a text version. Then try asking the same question with different words, and add a **Q&A** for the answer you expect.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The agent read pages I don't want">
    Select those rows and click **Delete**. Next time, uncheck them in the page list after **Find pages**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
