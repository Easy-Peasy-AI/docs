Your agent can answer in a Basecamp chat (Campfire). Your team types the chatbot's command followed by a question, and the agent replies in the chat. Basecamp is available on every plan.

## Add your agent to Basecamp

<Steps>
  <Step title="Copy the Command URL">
    Open your agent, click **Channels**, and click **Connect** on **Basecamp Chatbots**. Copy the **Command URL**. Keep it private: anyone with it can send questions to your agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a chatbot in Basecamp">
    In Basecamp, open the chat you want, click the **⋯** menu at the top right, and choose **Configure chatbots**. Click **Add a new chatbot**, give it a name, such as `helper`, an optional avatar, and paste the **Command URL**. Click **Add this chatbot**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask a question">
    In the chat, type `!helper` followed by your question, using the name you chose. The agent replies in a few seconds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- The agent only answers when someone uses its command; it doesn't post on its own.
- You can add several agents to one chat, each with its own name, such as `!hr` and `!support`.
- Each agent accepts up to 30 commands every 10 minutes from one place.
- To remove it, delete the chatbot in **Configure chatbots**.
