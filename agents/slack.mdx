Your agent can answer your team in Slack: in direct messages, and in channels when someone @mentions it. It's a quick way to give everyone access to your handbook, policies, or product docs. Slack is available on every plan, including Free.

<Note>
  You need permission to install apps in your Slack workspace, or a workspace admin who approves it.
</Note>

## Add your agent to Slack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    Open your agent, click **Channels**, and click **Connect** on **Slack**. Slack's authorization page opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow access">
    Pick your workspace at the top right, choose the channel to post in, and click **Allow**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask it something">
    In Slack, find the app under **Apps** in the sidebar and send it a message. To use it in a channel, add the app to the channel and @mention it with your question.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How it works

- In direct messages the agent answers every message. In channels it answers when it's @mentioned, in a thread.
- Each person's conversation keeps its history. Type `/clear` to start over.
- Your [actions](/agents/actions) work in Slack.

Once connected, open the Slack card to see **Manage Slack Workspaces**: each workspace with its channel, its status, and a switch to pause it. **Connect a New Slack Workspace** adds another workspace.

## Tips

- Train the agent on your internal documents: handbook, IT guides, benefits, product specs.
- Create a channel such as `#ask-the-agent` and add the app there, so answers are visible to everyone.
- In the [instructions](/agents/instructions-and-model#instructions), say where to send people when the agent doesn't know, for example "Point people to #it-helpdesk."
