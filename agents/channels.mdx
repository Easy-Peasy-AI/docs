One agent can answer on many channels at once, with the same knowledge, instructions, and actions everywhere. Open your agent and click **Channels** to connect them.

## Available channels

| Channel | How people reach your agent | Plans |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Website](/agents/website) | A chat bubble, a shareable link, or a chat inside a page | All |
| [Website platforms](/agents/website-platforms) | WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Webflow, and other site builders | All |
| [Telegram](/agents/telegram) | Messages to your Telegram bot | All |
| [Facebook Messenger](/agents/messenger) | Messages to your Facebook Page | All |
| [Slack](/agents/slack) | Direct messages and @mentions in your workspace | All |
| [WhatsApp](/agents/whatsapp) | Messages to your WhatsApp Business number | Paid |
| [LINE](/agents/line) | Messages to your LINE Official Account | Paid |
| [Email](/agents/email) | Emails to the agent's own address | Paid |
| [Phone calls](/agents/phone-calls) | Calls to a Twilio phone number | Paid |
| [Shopify](/agents/shopify) | The chat on your store, with product search and order status | Paid |
| [Basecamp](/agents/basecamp) | Commands in a Basecamp chat | All |
| [REST API](/agents/integrations#rest-api) and [Zapier](/agents/integrations#zapier) | Your own apps and automations | All |

On the Free plan, the paid channels show **Pro** and an **Upgrade** button.

## Connect a channel

Click **Connect** on a channel's card. Most channels open a window where you sign in or paste a key; each channel's page has the steps. The book icon on a card opens its setup guide.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/agents/channels-messaging.webp" alt="Messaging & voice channel cards: Telegram, WhatsApp, LINE, Phone Calls (Twilio), Shopify, Email, Facebook Messenger, Slack, WordPress, and Basecamp Chatbots, each with a Connect button" />
</Frame>

Once connected, a card shows **Connected**. **Connected** at the top of the page filters to your connected channels, and the search box finds a channel by name.

## Pause a channel

Open a connected channel and switch off **Enable integration**. The agent stops answering on that channel until you switch it back on, and the card shows **Paused**. Your other channels keep working.

## What works where

| | Website chat | WhatsApp, Messenger, Slack, LINE, Email, Basecamp | Telegram | Phone calls | REST API |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Knowledge and instructions | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| [Actions](/agents/actions) | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| [Image attachments](/agents/appearance#image-attachments) | Yes | No | Yes | — | No |
| [Voice calls](/agents/voice) | Yes | No | No | Yes | No |
| [Lead form](/agents/leads) | Yes | No | No | No | No |

Buttons, forms, and suggested messages are made for the website chat. [Conversations](/agents/conversations) lists chats from the website, WhatsApp, Messenger, LINE, and Telegram.
