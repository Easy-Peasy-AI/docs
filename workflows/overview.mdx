---
title: "Workflows"
description: "Chain AI steps on a visual canvas, from text to images, videos, speech, and music, and reuse the whole chain with new inputs."
---

**Workflows** let you connect AI steps on a canvas, so the result of one step feeds the next. Write a script, turn it into images, animate them, add a voiceover, and merge the clips, then change the first step and run it all again.

Each step is a **node**: a text input, an image or video model, a tool, text to speech, and more. You build the chain once and reuse it whenever you need the same kind of result.

<Note>
  Workflows are in beta, and the editor works on desktop screens only.
</Note>

## Open Workflows

Click **Workflows** in the sidebar, or find it in **All Tools** > **AI Agents**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/workflows-page.webp" alt="AI Workflow Builder, Design powerful, automated multi-step AI workflows for images, video, and more, with Create Workflow, Browse Templates, and Import Workflow buttons, How to Start, and the My Workflows and Templates tabs" />
</Frame>

- **My Workflows** lists your workflows. Search finds them by name.
- **Shared with me** lists workflows other people shared with you. It appears once someone does.
- **Templates** has ready-made workflows for images and videos, such as virtual try-on, virtual staging, a product commercial, and an animated short film.

**How to Start** plays a short video tour.

## Create a workflow

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="From scratch">
    Click **Create Workflow**. An empty canvas opens, named with today's date. Click the name at the top to rename it. See [Build and run a workflow](/workflows/build).
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From a template">
    Click **Browse Templates**, then a template to preview it. Click **Use Template** at the top right to copy it into your workflows, then change the inputs and run it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="From a file">
    Click **Import Workflow** and choose a workflow `.json` file exported from Easy-Peasy.AI.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Manage your workflows

Click the ⋯ menu on a workflow's card:

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/workflows/workflow-card-menu.webp" alt="A workflow card, Class promo image, with its menu open: Edit, Duplicate, Export, Add to favorites, Upgrade to Teams, and Delete" width="370" />
</Frame>

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit** | Opens the workflow |
| **Duplicate** | Makes a copy, including the results already in its nodes |
| **Export** | Downloads the workflow as a `.json` file, to back it up or import it into another account |
| **Share with Team** | On a Teams plan, shares the workflow with your team. On other plans this reads **Upgrade to Teams** |
| **Delete** | Deletes the workflow. Only its owner can |

Workflows save automatically as you edit.

## What workflows cost

Workflows have no charge of their own, and there's no limit on how many you make. Each node uses the same allowance as the feature it runs: image and video nodes use image & video credits, text nodes use words, and speech and music use text-to-speech characters. Moving data between nodes, splitting images, and merging videos are free. See [Build and run a workflow](/workflows/build#what-each-node-costs).
