**AI Dubbing** translates the speech in a video or audio file and re-voices it in another language. Each speaker keeps a voice that sounds like their own, and the delivery follows the original timing. Background sound and music stay in place.

Use it to publish videos, courses, podcasts, and ads in more languages without re-recording.

## Dub a file

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Dubbing">
    Click **Audio** > **AI Dubbing** in the top menu, or find it in **All Tools**. The bar at the top shows how many text-to-speech characters you've used.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your file or link">
    Drag a video or audio file into **Upload File or Enter URL**, up to 1 GB and 2.5 hours. Or paste a link to a video on YouTube, X, TikTok, Vimeo, and other sites.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/audio/dubbing-form.webp" alt="AI Dubbing with front-desk-call.mp3 uploaded successfully, the URL field, Project Name Front desk call, From English, To Spanish, and Advanced Settings" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the languages">
    Give the project a name if you like. Set **From** to the language spoken in the file and **To** to the language you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the settings (optional)">
    Open **Advanced Settings**:

    - **Number of Speakers**: how many people speak. Leave it empty to detect them automatically.
    - **Highest resolution**: keeps the video at its best quality, for a larger file.

    **Character Usage** shows an estimate for your file.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/audio/dubbing-settings.webp" alt="Advanced Settings with Number of Speakers 2 and Highest resolution off, Character Usage of about 2000 characters per minute from your Text-to-Speech quota, about 793 characters for this file, and the Create Dubbing button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create Dubbing">
    The project appears under **Recent Projects** as **Processing...**. Short files are ready in under a minute; long videos can take much longer. When it says **Ready**, click the download button to save the dubbed file.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/audio/dubbing-result.webp" alt="Recent Projects with Front desk call, English to Spanish, 794 characters, marked Ready, with a download button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can leave the page while a project is processing. Open **AI Dubbing** again to see its status and download it. **Recent Projects** shows your 20 most recent projects.

## Languages

Dub from and to any of these 32 languages: Arabic, Bulgarian, Chinese, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Filipino, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hindi, Hungarian, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Norwegian, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Slovak, Spanish, Swedish, Tamil, Turkish, Ukrainian, and Vietnamese.

## Tips

- **Start with clear speech.** Dubbing works best when speakers don't talk over each other and the music is quieter than the voices.
- **Set the number of speakers** when you know it. It helps keep each person's voice separate.
- **Try a short clip first.** Check the result on a minute of your video before dubbing the whole thing.

## What it costs

AI Dubbing uses your plan's text-to-speech characters: about 2,000 per minute of the original file. The estimate is shown before you start, and the final count is based on the file's length. See [Audio plans and limits](/audio/limits).
