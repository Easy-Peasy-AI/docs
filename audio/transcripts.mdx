## The transcript

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/audio/stt-transcript.webp" alt="Speakers Heather and Daniel, and the transcription with each line labeled by speaker with start and end times, a Download menu, a Copy button, and Delete transcription" />
</Frame>

- **Speakers**: replace Speaker A, Speaker B, and so on with real names. The names appear in the transcript and help AI Content write accurate summaries.
- **Play from a line**: click the speaker icon next to a name to play from there. The words are highlighted as they're spoken.
- **Copy** copies the whole transcript with speaker names.
- **Download** saves subtitles in **SRT Format** or **VTT Format**, with timings, for video editors and YouTube.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/audio/stt-download.webp" alt="The Download menu with SRT Format and VTT Format" width="360" />
</Frame>

When speaker detection is off, the tab is called **Plain Text** and shows the text without speakers or times.

The transcript text can't be edited. To fix names and terms in what you publish, edit the AI Content or copy the text into your editor.

The player at the bottom plays the recording, skips 10 seconds back or forward, changes the playback speed, and downloads the recording.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/audio/stt-player.webp" alt="The player bar with play, 10-second back and forward buttons, a progress bar at 00 of 23 seconds, and speed, volume, and download buttons" />
</Frame>

## AI Content

**AI Content** turns the transcript into ready-to-use text. The first time you open it, a summary and the other items for your type of recording are written automatically.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/audio/stt-ai-content.webp" alt="AI Content with a Phone Call Summary: an overview of Heather's call with Daniel, a first-time student who booked the free Sunrise Flow class, followed by Key Points" />
</Frame>

| Type of recording | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Every type except Therapy Session | Summary, Title, Timestamped Overview, Topics and Bullets, Description, LinkedIn Post, Keywords |
| Podcast | Also show notes, a Twitter thread, a newsletter, an article, and more |
| Meeting | Also Action Items |
| Therapy Session | Summary, Progress Note, SOAP Note, DAP Note, Topics and Bullets, Keywords |

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/audio/stt-ai-content-list.webp" alt="The AI Content items collapsed: Summary, Title, Timestamped Overview, Topics and Bullets, Description, and LinkedIn Post" />
</Frame>

Each item has **Generate** to write it again and **Copy**. **Collapse All** and **Expand All** fold the list. AI Content uses words.

## AI Chat

**AI Chat** answers questions about the recording: what was decided, who said what, or a draft of a follow-up email.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/audio/stt-ai-chat.webp" alt="AI Chat: the question Which class did Daniel book, and does he need to bring anything? and the answer that he booked the Sunrise Flow class on Monday at 6:30 and doesn't need to bring anything" />
</Frame>

AI Chat reads about the first 10,000 tokens of the transcript, roughly 45 minutes of conversation. For longer recordings, ask about the beginning, or use AI Content, which reads more. AI Chat uses words.

## Rename, share, and delete

- **Rename**: click the pencil next to the title, type, and press Enter.
- **Share**: click **Share** at the top right. **Share Publicly** creates a public page with the transcript and AI Content. On a Teams plan, **Share with Team Members** shows it in your teammates' lists.
- **Delete**: click **Delete transcription** at the bottom and confirm. The recording is deleted and can't be restored. Deleting doesn't give the transcription back to your allowance. Turn off **Share Publicly** before you delete.
