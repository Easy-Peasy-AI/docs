**Sound Effect Generator** makes sound effects and background ambience from a description: footsteps on gravel, rain on a window, a door creaking open, a sci-fi engine hum. It makes sounds only, never speech or music; for a voiceover or a song, use [text to speech](/audio/text-to-speech) or the [music generator](/audio/music).

## Generate a sound effect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Sound Effect Generator">
    Click **Audio** > **Sound Effect Generator** in the top menu, or find it in **All Tools**. The number at the top right shows how many text-to-speech characters you have left.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the sound">
    Type it in **Describe the sound effect you want**, up to 2,000 characters. Say what makes the sound, where it happens, what it sounds like, and how it changes over time. **Ambience**, **Foley**, and **Sci-fi** fill in an example to start from.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/audio/sound-effects-form.webp" alt="AI Sound Effect Generator with the description A Tibetan singing bowl struck once in a quiet room, the warm tone ringing out and slowly fading to silence, the Ambience, Foley, and Sci-fi examples, Target duration 8 sec, Download format WAV for editing, and Generate sound" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the length and format">
    - **Target duration**: 1 to 30 seconds. The AI aims for it; the actual length can differ a little.
    - **Download format**: **WAV** for editing, or **MP3** for a smaller file.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Generate sound">
    The sound is ready in about 20 seconds and appears under **Generated Sound Effects**.

    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your sound effects

Each sound has **Play**, **Share**, **Details**, and **Download**. **Details** shows the description, the date, and the characters used, and has **Delete**. **Share** works like it does for [speech](/audio/text-to-speech): **Share Publicly** makes a public link.

The page shows your 18 most recent sounds. **History** lists the rest.

## Tips for descriptions

- **Name the source and the surface.** "Boots walking slowly on wet gravel" works better than "footsteps".
- **Describe the space.** A small room, a cathedral, an open field, underwater.
- **Describe changes over time.** "Rain starts softly, gets heavier, then a distant thunder roll."
- **Keep one scene per sound.** Generate separate sounds and layer them in your editor.

## What it costs

Each sound uses 1,000 text-to-speech characters, whatever its length. Characters are used only when a sound is saved, so a failed generation costs nothing. You need at least 1,000 characters left to generate; otherwise the button changes to **Upgrade plan**. The Free plan's 100 characters aren't enough for a sound effect. See [Audio plans and limits](/audio/limits).

You can make one sound at a time, together with the [audio generator](/audio/audio-generator).

## For developers

Generate sound effects from your own code with `POST /api/generate-sound`. See [Generate sound effect](/api-reference/endpoint/generate-sound).
