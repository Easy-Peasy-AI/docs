**AI Music Generator** writes and produces songs: vocals, instruments, and cover art. Describe the song you want, or write your own lyrics and style. Each generation gives you two versions to choose from.

Use it for jingles, background music for videos and podcasts, birthday songs, and personal gifts.

## Make a song

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Music Generator">
    Click **AI Music Generator** in the sidebar, or **Audio** > **Music Generator** in the top menu. **Usage** at the top shows how many text-to-speech characters you've used this period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a model">
    **V6** is the default. You can also pick **V6 Wild** or **V6 Mini** and compare; all three cost the same.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the song">
    With **Custom Mode** off, describe it in **Song Description**, up to 400 characters: the genre, mood, what it's about, and any names to include. The lyrics are written for you.

    For example: "An upbeat acoustic song for a yoga studio's anniversary, about morning classes, community, and breathing easy."

    Not sure where to start? Pick one under **Start with a template**, or click **Surprise me** for a random idea. Both fill in the description, and you can edit it before you generate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the vocals">
    Tick **Instrumental Only** for music without singing. Otherwise, set **Vocal Gender** to **Auto**, **Male**, or **Female**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Generate">
    **Generate** stays at the bottom of the form while you scroll. The song takes a few minutes. Two versions appear under **Music**, each with its own cover.

</Steps>

## Start from a template

Templates are ready-made sounds, from a modern pop anthem or a 90s R&B slow jam to lo-fi study beats, a birthday song, or a podcast intro. Each one has a 30-second preview made with that exact style.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a template">
    The most popular ones are under **Start with a template**. Click **Browse all** to open the **Templates** tab, where you can filter by category or search.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Listen first">
    Click ▶ on a template to hear its preview. Previews don't use any characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use it">
    Click the template. With **Custom Mode** on, it fills in **Style**, **Exclude styles**, and the vocals, and leaves your lyrics as they are. With **Custom Mode** off, it fills in the song description. Click **Undo** in the message that appears to go back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you use an instrumental template while your lyrics are filled in, your lyrics are kept and the song has vocals.

## Write your own lyrics

Turn on **Custom Mode** to control the words and the sound:

- **Style**: genres, instruments, mood, and tempo, up to 1,000 characters (200 on the Free plan). For example, "Calm acoustic folk, soft fingerpicked guitar, gentle female vocals, 70 BPM". Click a tag under the field (**Genre**, **Mood**, **Vocals**, **Instruments**, **Tempo**, **Era & sound**) to add it, and click it again to remove it. **Recent** puts back a style you used before, and **Random style** picks a template's style.
- **Exclude styles** (optional): sounds you don't want, separated by commas, such as "autotune, heavy distortion, choir". The AI tries to avoid them, but it can't guarantee it.
- **Lyrics**: up to 3,000 characters. Mark the parts with tags such as `[Verse]`, `[Chorus]`, and `[Bridge]`: click a tag button above the field to add it where your cursor is. With the field empty, **Song structure** adds a full outline (intro, verses, choruses, bridge, outro) to write into.

## Your songs

Each song has **Play**, **Reuse prompt** (pencil), **Share**, **Details** (ⓘ), and **Download**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/audio/music-details.webp" alt="Song details: the cover, the text and lyrics, Created At, Characters Used 300, Status Completed, Generate Lyric Video, and Delete, Play, Share, and Download" width="500" />
</Frame>

- **Download** offers **MP3** (a smaller file, for sharing and listening) and **WAV** (uncompressed, for editing and mastering). The first WAV download of a song takes about a minute to prepare, and it starts on its own when it's ready; after that it downloads straight away. WAV doesn't use any characters.
- **Reuse prompt** loads the song's style and lyrics (or its description) back into the form, so you can change something and generate again.
- **Details** shows the lyrics, the date, and the characters used, and has **Delete**.
- **Generate Lyric Video** makes a simple video of the cover with the lyrics on screen. It's not a music video.
- **Share** makes a public link, like it does for [speech](/audio/text-to-speech). On a Teams plan you can also share with your team.

**Examples** shows songs other people have made; open **Details** on one to see its prompt, and click **Use this prompt** to load it into the form. All your songs are also listed in **History** > **Music**.

## What it costs

Each generation uses 600 text-to-speech characters: 300 for each of the two versions. If a song fails, it isn't counted. The Free plan's 100 characters are enough for one generation a month; paid plans include many more. See [Audio plans and limits](/audio/limits).
