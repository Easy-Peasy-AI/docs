---
title: "Buy more credits"
description: "Top up image & video credits or words when your plan allowance runs low, without changing your plan."
---

If you need more before your allowance resets, you can buy extra image & video credits, and on larger plans, extra words. What you buy is added to your account as **bonus credits** or **bonus words**.

<Note>
  Buying extra credits is for paid plans. On the Free plan, [upgrade](/account/subscription#upgrade-or-change-your-plan) instead.
</Note>

## Buy image & video credits

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Buy more credits">
    You'll find the button on [**Settings** > **Subscription**](https://easy-peasy.ai/settings/billing), on [**Settings** > **Usage**](https://easy-peasy.ai/settings/usage), and on the image and video pages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an option">
    **Purchase Options** lists one-time packs of 100 or 500 credits, and bigger plans you can upgrade to. A bigger plan usually costs less per credit, and the dialog shows the price per credit for each option.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/account/buy-credits-dialog.webp" alt="The Purchase Options dialog: plan upgrades with credits per month, and one-time purchases of 100 and 500 credits with a Buy Now button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Click **Buy Now** and pay on the Stripe checkout page. You can change the quantity there to buy up to 10 packs at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The credits appear as **Bonus credits** on the Usage page as soon as the payment goes through.

## Buy more words

On Unlimited 200 and larger plans, [**Settings** > **Usage**](https://easy-peasy.ai/settings/usage) shows **Buy more words** next to **Words Usage**. Each purchase adds 100,000 bonus words.

## Buy for a team member

If you own a team, you can buy credits or words for one member from [**Settings** > **Team**](https://easy-peasy.ai/settings/team). They go to that member's balance, not yours.

## How bonus credits and words are used

- They're used only after your plan's monthly allowance runs out.
- They don't reset. Whatever you don't use carries over to the next period.
- They show up separately on the Usage page as **Bonus credits** and **Bonus words**.

## Get bonus credits by referring friends

Share your link from [**Settings** > **Refer a friend**](https://easy-peasy.ai/settings/referrals). When someone you invite becomes a paying customer, you both get 50,000 bonus words and 150 bonus credits. If they choose Unlimited or a larger plan, you both get 100,000 words and 300 credits instead.
