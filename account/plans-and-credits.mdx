Every plan comes with a monthly allowance, split into a few separate balances. Each feature draws from one of them, and you can watch all of them on [**Settings** > **Usage**](/account/usage).

| Balance | What uses it | How it's counted |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Words** | Marky Agent, tools, and agents on some models | Words of text the AI writes, adjusted by the model's multiplier |
| **Image & video credits** | AI Images, AI Videos, talking videos, image editing, upscaling, headshots, interior design, and images or slides Marky makes | A price per image, or per second of video, set by each model |
| **Text-to-speech characters** | Text to speech, voice changer, dubbing, sound effects, the audio generator, and music | Characters of text you turn into speech; other audio features use a fixed amount per job |
| **Transcriptions** | AI Speech to Text and Record & Transcribe | One per file |
| **Agents** | Agents | How many agents you can have |

Compare what each paid plan includes on the [pricing page](https://easy-peasy.ai/pricing).

## Words

Marky Agent and the tools count the words the AI writes for you. Two things change the count:

- **The model's multiplier.** Every chat model has one, shown next to its name in Marky's model picker. A 0.5x model uses half a word per word it writes; a 2x model uses two. The default model is one of the cheapest.
- **What the model reads and thinks.** A model's reasoning counts as writing. Premium models such as Claude Opus and Claude Fable also charge for long conversations and large files they read, after a free allowance each time.

<Note>
  In the [Quickstart](/get-started/quickstart), Claude Opus 5.5 Reasoning wrote a 110-word email and used 505 words, because its reasoning and the conversation it read count too. The same request on the default model would use a small fraction of that.
</Note>

On the Unlimited and Teams plans, some models are labelled **Unlimited** in the model picker and don't use words at all. In tools, the **Standard model** is also free of words on those plans.

A few more rules:

- On the Free plan, every chat model counts at 0.2x.
- On the Starter plan, models labelled **Unlimited** count at 0.1x.
- Uploading an image to a tool adds 150 words.
- Agents built on some models use your words when they reply to your customers.

## Image & video credits

Images, videos, and the other visual features share one pool of credits. What a job costs depends on the model, the resolution, and for videos, the length. The **Generate** button always shows the cost before you start, and videos are priced per second: a 5-second clip at 4 credits per second costs 20 credits.

- **Failed jobs are refunded automatically.** If a generation fails, its credits come back without you doing anything.
- **You can refund a result you don't like.** Click the thumbs-down on your own image or video and its credits are returned, up to 10% of your plan's monthly credits each billing period.
- **Deleting doesn't refund.** Once an image or video has been created, deleting it doesn't return its credits.

## Text-to-speech characters

Text to speech uses one character per character of your script, half that with **Turbo**, and one per ten characters with OpenAI voices. Other audio features take a set amount from the same balance:

| Feature | Uses |
| --- | --- |
| Voice changer and dubbing | About 2,000 per minute of audio |
| Sound effects and the audio generator | 1,000 per generation |
| Music and songs | 600 per generation (two tracks, 300 each) |

## When your allowance resets

- **Paid plans** reset every month on your billing date. Yearly plans reset monthly too, on the same day of the month you subscribed.
- **The Free plan** resets on the 1st of each month.

Unused allowance doesn't roll over. Credits and words you [buy separately](/account/buy-credits) are different: they're used only after your plan allowance runs out, and whatever you don't use carries over to the next period.

## The Free plan

New accounts start on the Free plan. It's for trying things out:

| Balance | Free plan |
| --- | --- |
| Words | 2,000 per month |
| Image & video credits | 3 per month |
| Text-to-speech characters | 100 per month |
| Transcriptions | 2 per month |
| Agents | 1, answering up to 100 messages per 30 days |

On the Free plan:

- Tools use the **Standard model** and make one result at a time. Tools marked as paid can be tried once.
- Premium image and video models are locked, and videos are limited to a few models and short lengths.
- Image downloads are for paid plans. Free accounts can download while they still have bonus credits.

## What happens when you run out

| You run out of | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Words | On Free and Starter, Marky and tools stop until your allowance resets. On Unlimited and Teams plans, you can keep using the **Unlimited** chat models and the **Standard model** in tools. |
| Image & video credits | You can't start new images or videos until your allowance resets. |
| Anything else | That feature pauses until your allowance resets. |

To keep going sooner, [buy more credits](/account/buy-credits) or [change your plan](/account/subscription).
