---
title: "HIPAA and the BAA"
description: "Sign our Business Associate Addendum to use the clinical note tools with patient information, with zero retention of their results."
---

If you work with protected health information in the US, sign the Easy-Peasy.AI **Business Associate Addendum (BAA)** before using it in the clinical note tools. Signing designates your account as a HIPAA account and turns on zero retention for those tools.

The BAA is available on paid plans.

## Sign the BAA

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the BAA">
    Go to [easy-peasy.ai/settings/baa](https://easy-peasy.ai/settings/baa), or click the HIPAA banner on a clinical note tool, such as the SOAP Note Generator.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read it">
    Read the agreement in the page, or click **Download BAA** to save a PDF copy. It lists the services it covers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tick the three boxes">
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/account/baa-acceptance.webp" alt="BAA Acceptance with three checkboxes: I acknowledge that I have read and understood this agreement, I take responsibility for the terms, and I agree to the Zero-Retention policy, which deletes content from the Psychotherapy Progress Note, SOAP Note, DAP Note, Treatment Plan, and BIRP Note Generators within 24 hours, and the Accept EASY-PEASY.AI BAA and Designate HIPAA Account button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Accept EASY-PEASY.AI BAA and Designate HIPAA Account">
    A **HIPAA BAA** tab appears in **Settings**, with the date you accepted and a copy to download.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Zero retention

Once you've signed, results from these tools are deleted automatically within 24 hours:

- Psychotherapy Progress Note Generator
- SOAP Note Generator
- DAP Note Generator
- Treatment Plan Generator
- BIRP Note Generator

Copy or save each note into your own records system as soon as it's written. After 24 hours it's gone from your History.

<Warning>
  Only use protected health information in the services the BAA covers. Other features, such as Marky Agent, AI Speech to Text, and agents, aren't part of it.
</Warning>

## End the BAA

On the **HIPAA BAA** tab, click **Terminate BAA Agreement** and confirm. Your account is no longer a HIPAA account, and new results from the note tools are kept like any others. You can sign the BAA again later on the same page.
