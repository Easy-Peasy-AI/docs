You can go from an idea to a finished image in under a minute. Describe what you want, choose how it should look, and let one of the models draw it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the image">
    Type what you want to see in **Prompt**. Say what's in the picture, where it is, the light, and the style. For example: "A bright yoga studio at sunrise: wooden floor, rolled mats, tall windows with golden light, potted plants, calm and airy, photorealistic."

    Two buttons in the box can help. **Dictate** (the microphone) lets you speak your prompt. The button next to it writes a **Random prompt** when the box is empty, or rewrites yours in more detail (**Improve prompt**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a style, camera, lighting, or shot (optional)">
    The **Customize** buttons add ready-made directions to your prompt:

    - **Style**: 43 looks, from **Oil Painting** and **Watercolor** to **Anime** and **Cyberpunk**.
    - **Camera**: presets such as **Cinematic** or **Phone Snap**, or a specific camera body, lens, and aperture.
    - **Lighting** and **Shot Type**: the light and the framing, such as **Golden Hour** light or a **Low Angle** shot.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-images/style-picker.webp" alt="The Choose a style window with sample images for Oil Painting, Digital art, Detailed photo, Cyberpunk, Artstation, Watercolor, Pencil drawing, Concept art, Cartoon, and Anime" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a model">
    Click the model under **Model** to open **Choose a model**. Every card says what the model is good at and what it costs per image. Search by name, or turn on **Multi-select** to generate the same prompt with several models at once and compare them. Compared images come out square, and the credits of each model add up.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-images/model-picker.webp" alt="The Choose a model window with model cards for Nano Banana 2 Lite, Nano Banana 2, Nano Banana Pro, Muse Image, Grok Imagine 2.0, and Grok, each with a description and a credit price" />
    </Frame>

    The default is **GPT Image 2.5 Flare Medium**, a good all-rounder at 3 credits. See [Image models and credits](/ai-images/models) for all of them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the size and how many">
    At the bottom of the panel, pick an aspect ratio, such as **1:1 Square**, **3:2 Standard**, or **16:9 Widescreen**, and how many images to make. You can make up to 4 at once, or 8 with some FLUX models. Models that offer it also show a **Resolution** setting, such as 1K, 2K, or 4K.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Generate">
    The button shows the total price, for example **Generate (3 credits)**. Your images appear in the **Images** tab on the right, usually within a minute.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Put your own people, products, or photos in the picture

Use **Upload**, **Character**, or **Object** to add a photo. **Character** and **Object** keep a library of the people and products you add, so you can reuse them for consistent results across many images. You can also pick any of your past images from **History**.

When a photo is attached, the page switches to editing: an **Action** menu appears, set to **Edit Image with AI**, and your prompt describes what to make from the photo. See [Edit images](/ai-images/edit).

**Photo Packs** turns a model you've trained on one person into themed photo sets, such as LinkedIn headshots or Halloween photos, from 3 credits per photo. It's available on paid plans.

## Tips for better images

- Be specific about the subject, the setting, and the light. "A golden retriever on a beach at sunset, low angle, warm light" beats "a dog".
- Put words you want to see in the image in quotes, and pick a model that's good at text, such as GPT Image 2.5 or Seedream 5.0 Pro.
- Try the same prompt with **Multi-select** to see which model suits it best, then stick with that one.
- To fix one detail, [edit the image](/ai-images/edit) instead of generating again.

## On the Free plan

- One image at a time. Most wide and tall sizes are locked; with the default model, only the square **1:1** size is available.
- Models marked **Paid plans** in the picker are locked, and so are 4K, **Multi-select**, and Midjourney.
- Downloading needs a paid plan or bonus credits. See [Plans and credits](/account/plans-and-credits#the-free-plan).

## If a generation fails or is blocked

- A tile that says **Generation Failed** doesn't cost anything. Try again, or try another model.
- If the GPT Image models refuse a prompt for policy reasons, we remake it with Seedream 5.0 Pro automatically. The tile then shows Seedream 5.0 Pro and is charged at that model's price.
- Uploaded photos are checked too. If one breaks the content policy, the button changes to **Content Blocked**.
