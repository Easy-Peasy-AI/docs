**Headshots** turns a few ordinary photos of you into a set of professional business headshots in different outfits, backgrounds, and lighting, for LinkedIn, your website, resumes, and company pages. It's a one-time purchase, separate from your plan, and available on every plan, including Free.

## Choose a package

Click **Images** > **Headshots** in the top menu, then **Get Your Headshots Now**.

## Order your headshots

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Click the package's button and pay at checkout. After paying, you come back to upload your photos.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload 3 to 8 photos">
    Use recent photos of yourself alone, PNG or JPEG, at least 500 × 500 pixels, from different angles. Before you continue, a checklist asks you to confirm the photos meet the requirements:

    - no sunglasses, and only you in the photo,
    - not too close or too far from the camera,
    - looking at the camera, with a natural expression,
    - sharp, not blurry.

    <Warning>
      Photos that don't meet the requirements give poor results, and the order can't be refunded.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your details">
    Enter your **Name**, **Age Range**, **Eye Color**, and **Gender**, and any **Additional Features**, such as glasses, a hijab, a turban, piercings, or your hair length. These help the headshots look like you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for your headshots">
    Your order shows **Generating headshots** on the Headshots page. It usually takes up to 2 hours; we email you when they're ready.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Download and use your headshots

Open **Headshots** and click **View Results** on the order.

- **Download All Photos** downloads them all in a ZIP file.
- On each photo: **Edit image**, **Download image**, **Upscale image**, and **Generate Talking Video**.
- **Run the model in Image Generator** opens [AI Images](/ai-images/photo-studio#create-photos-from-a-prompt) with you as the model, to create more photos of you from a prompt.

<Warning>
  Headshots are deleted automatically 30 days after they're ready. Download the ones you want to keep.
</Warning>

To order again, click **Create another headshot**. To delete an order and its photos, use its ⋯ menu.

## Team headshots

Order headshots for your whole team, with consistent styles, and pay once.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the team size">
    Switch pricing to **Team** and set **Team Size**, from 3 to 50 people. Each person costs $29, with a discount of 10% from 10 people, 20% from 20, and 30% from 30. The team size can't change after checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Click **Get Headshots for Your Team**. After paying, click **Manage Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    On **Headshots Team**, click **Invite a member** and send the invitation by email or link. When someone joins, their order is ready for them to upload photos, and their status changes to **Model Created** once they have.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The owner sees each member's status, can start an order for a member, and can remove members. Members see their order marked **Team Shared**.

## What it costs

Headshots are a one-time payment in US dollars, charged at checkout. They don't use your plan's credits. Editing, upscaling, and talking videos made from your headshots use [image & video credits](/account/plans-and-credits) as usual.
