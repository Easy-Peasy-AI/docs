---
title: "Remove or replace a background"
description: "Cut the subject out of any image, or place it in front of a new background you describe."
---

## Remove a background

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="An image you made">
    Open the image from **Images** or **History** > **Images** and click **Remove Background**. The cut-out is saved as a new image in **History** > **Images**, and the original stays as it is. It costs 1 credit.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-images/remove-background-result.webp" alt="The result of Remove Background: a laptop and desk cut out onto a transparent background, shown as black, with Credits used: 1" />
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Your own photo">
    1. Choose **Images** > **Remove Background** from the menu at the top.
    2. Drop your photo into **Upload Image**.
    3. Click **Generate**.

    It costs 1 credit, and the result appears in **Images**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

The result has a transparent background. Image viewers that don't support transparency show it as black or checkered, but it's transparent when you place it on a slide, website, or design.

## Replace a background

1. Choose **Images** > **Replace Background** from the menu at the top.
2. Upload your photo.
3. In **Prompt**, describe the new background, for example "a white marble countertop in a bright kitchen".
4. Click **Generate**.

The subject stays as it is and the background is replaced. It costs 2 credits per image.

<Tip>
  For product photos, describe the surface, the light, and the setting: "on a wooden table by a window, soft morning light, shallow depth of field". A specific background looks more natural than a vague one.
</Tip>

## Other ways to change a background

- The **Change Background** and **Product Background Change** templates do the same job with a tested prompt. See [Templates and one-click tools](/ai-images/templates-and-tools).
- To change the background and something else at the same time, [edit the image](/ai-images/edit) and describe both changes.
