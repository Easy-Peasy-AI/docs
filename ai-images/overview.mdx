Open **AI Images** in the sidebar, or choose **Images** > **Image Generator** from the menu at the top of any tool page. The page has two halves:

- **On the left**, you describe the image, add your own photos if you want them in it, pick a model, and click **Generate**.
- **On the right**, you see your results and ideas to start from.

| Tab on the right | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Images** | Your latest results. Everything older is in **History** > **Images**. |
| **Templates** | Ready-made looks you can reuse with your own photos, such as mockups, posters, and portrait styles. |
| **AI Image Tools** | One-click jobs such as **Remove Background**, **Ghiblify**, and **Caricature**. |
| **Examples** | Images other people chose to share publicly, for inspiration. |

## More image tools

The **Images** menu at the top of the page also opens these:

| Tool | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Image Editor** | A full-page version of the editor. See [Edit images](/ai-images/edit). |
| **Magic Upscaler** | See [Upscale images](/ai-images/upscale). |
| **Headshots** | Professional headshots of you or your team from a few selfies. |
| **AI Photo Studio** | Train your own model from photos of a person, then generate themed photo packs with it. |
| **Interior Designer** | Redesign a room from a photo. |
| **Remove Background** and **Replace Background** | See [Remove or replace a background](/ai-images/remove-background). |

## Credits

Every image uses image & video credits. The price depends on the model and, for some models, the resolution. The **Generate** button shows the total before you start. See [Image models and credits](/ai-images/models) for every model's price, and [Plans and credits](/account/plans-and-credits) for how credits work.
