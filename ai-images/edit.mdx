You don't have to start over to change one thing. Edit an image you made, or upload your own, and describe what should be different.

## Open the editor

There are three ways in, and they all work the same:

- **From a result.** Hover over an image and click the pencil, or open it and click **Edit image**.
- **On its own page.** Choose **Images** > **Image Editor** from the menu at the top. Upload an image (JPG, PNG, or WebP) or pick one of your recent images.
- **From your own photo.** On AI Images, add a photo with **Upload**, **Character**, or **Object**. The **Action** menu appears, set to **Edit Image with AI**. Describe the change in **Prompt** and click **Generate**.

## Describe the change

The editor has two ways to say what you want:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Prompt">
    Type the change in **What do you want to change?**, as if you were asking a designer. For example: "Make it evening and turn on the lamps" or "Change the mats to deep blue".
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Visual">
    Click **Visual**, then click the spot on the image you want to change. A numbered marker appears; type what should happen there and click **Done**. Add as many markers as you need. This is the easiest way to change one object without touching the rest.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-images/edit-visual-marker.webp" alt="Visual mode in the Edit Image window: marker 1 on a plant by the wall, with the instruction 'Replace this plant with a small wooden sign that says Sunrise Yoga'" />
    </Frame>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Then choose an **AI Model** and a size. **Auto** keeps the shape of your original. Click **Generate**; the button shows the price, for example **Generate • 3 credits**.

## Keep editing, version by version

Each edit adds a new version to the strip under the controls. Click **Original** or any version to see it, and keep editing from whichever one you like best. Every version is also saved in **History** > **Images**.

## Add reference images

Click the paperclip to add other images the model should use, such as a product to place in the scene or a person to put in the picture. Only models that can combine several images are listed while references are attached: the FLUX.2, Seedream, Nano Banana, and GPT Image families.

## Limits

- Upload JPG, PNG, or WebP files. On AI Images you can attach up to 8 photos at once. Very large images are scaled down to 3,840 pixels on the long side, and transparent areas become white.
- Uploads are checked against our content policy. If one is blocked, the **Generate** button changes to **Content Blocked**.
- There's no brush or mask. To change one area, use **Visual** markers.
