When you don't want to write a prompt from scratch, start from a template or a tool. Both live in the right half of **AI Images**.

## Use a template

Templates are ready-made looks: product mockups, posters, portrait styles, filters, and more. Each one comes with a tested prompt, the model it was made for, and a sample photo you swap for your own.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find a template">
    Open the **Templates** tab and filter by category, such as **New**, **Starring You**, **Product**, or **Mockups**. Hover over a template and click **Use template**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Swap in your photo">
    The left panel fills in: the template appears at the top, with its prompt and model below it. Its sample photo sits in the **Customize** row. Remove the sample with its X, then add your own photo with **Upload**, **Character**, or **Object**, or pick one of your past images from **History**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-images/template-loaded.webp" alt="The Laptop Screen Mockup template loaded in the left panel, with its prompt, the yoga studio image added as image1 in the Customize row, and a Generate (3 credits) button" width="472" />
    </Frame>

    Templates in **Starring You** need a photo of a person. Product templates need a photo of the product.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate">
    Change the prompt if you like, then click **Generate**. This example put the yoga studio image on a laptop screen:

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-images/template-result.webp" alt="A laptop on a wooden desk showing the yoga studio image on its screen, with books, a mug, and plants around it" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

To stop using a template, click the X on the template card at the top of the panel. It clears what the template filled in and keeps the rest.

<Tip>
  When you switch from one template to another, check the **Customize** row for a photo left over from the previous template, and remove it if you don't need it.
</Tip>

## One-click tools

The **AI Image Tools** tab lists jobs that need only a photo. Some also appear in the **Action** menu that shows up when you add a photo on AI Images.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/ai-images/ai-image-tools.webp" alt="The AI Image Tools tab with cards for Train my AI Model, Visualize What Happens Next, Hyper Realistic Photos, Edit Image with AI, Turn photo into muppets, Ghiblify, and more" />
</Frame>

| Tool | What it does | Credits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Remove Background** | Cuts the subject out onto a transparent background | 1 |
| **Replace Background** | Puts the subject in front of a new background you describe | 2 per image |
| **Caricature** | Turns a portrait into a caricature | 2 per image |
| **Ghiblify** | Redraws a photo as Studio Ghibli–style artwork | 2 per image |
| **Turn photo into muppets** | Turns the people in a photo into puppets | 2 per image |
| **Hyper Realistic Photos** | Makes a photo look more realistic and detailed | 2 per image |
| **Visualize What Happens Next** | Draws a 3×3 grid of scenes that continue the story of your image | 12 |
| **Stylization** | Makes eight stylized portraits at once, such as manga, claymation, oil painting, and sketch. Paid plans | 16 |
| **Colorize** (in the **Action** menu) | Adds color to a black-and-white photo | 8 per image |
| **Character Reference** (in the **Action** menu) | Puts the same person into a new scene | 8 per image |

These cards open other tools instead:

- **Edit Image with AI**, **Sketch to Image**, and **Style Transfer** open AI Images ready to edit. Add your photo and describe the result you want. See [Edit images](/ai-images/edit).
- **Creative Upscale** opens the [Magic Upscaler](/ai-images/upscale).
- **Animate Image** opens [AI Videos](/videos/generate) to turn the photo into a video.
- **Headshot Generator**, **Train my AI Model**, and **Interior & Exterior Design** open [Headshots](/ai-images/headshots), [AI Photo Studio](/ai-images/photo-studio), and [Interior Designer](/ai-images/interior-designer).
