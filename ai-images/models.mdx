You choose a model in **Choose a model** on [AI Images](/ai-images/generate). This page lists the same models, in the same order, with the same prices.

## How prices work

- **Per image.** The price is for one image. Making four images costs four times as much.
- **By resolution.** Some models charge more for bigger images, for example 4 credits at 1K and 8 credits at 4K. Pick the resolution under **Resolution**.
- **Shown before you start.** The **Generate** button shows the total for your settings.

The default model is **GPT Image 2.5 Flare Medium** at 3 credits per image.

## Your own models

Models you train in **AI Photo Studio** appear under **My Custom Models** in **Choose a model**. Use them to generate photos of the person you trained them on.
