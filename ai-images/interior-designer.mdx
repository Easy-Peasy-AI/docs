**Interior Designer** reimagines rooms, gardens, and building exteriors. Upload a photo and say what to change, try it in a new style, furnish an empty room, or design a space from nothing. It's used by homeowners, real estate agents, designers, and anyone planning a makeover.

## Choose a mode

Click **AI Interior Designer** in the sidebar, or **Images** > **Interior Designer** in the top menu. Pick what to do under **Choose mode**:

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/ai-images/interior-modes.webp" alt="The Choose mode menu: Edit, perfect for changing colors, materials, or adding and removing specific items; Restyle, choose from various design themes to transform your existing space; Generate, start from scratch with AI-generated room designs; 360° Panorama; and Declutter Room" width="450" />
</Frame>

| Mode | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit** | Changes what you describe: colors, materials, furniture, decor |
| **Restyle** | Redesigns your room in one or more design themes |
| **Generate** | Designs a room from scratch, without a photo |
| **360° Panorama** | Makes a 360° image of a room you can look around in |
| **Declutter Room** | Removes clutter and loose items |
| **Virtual Staging** | Furnishes an empty room |
| **Empty Room** | Removes the furniture |
| **Color / Paint Changer** | Repaints the walls in the color you describe |

## Edit a room

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload a photo">
    Drag in a photo of the room, garden, or exterior: JPG, PNG, or WebP, up to 10 MB. For **Edit** and **360° Panorama**, **Add more** adds up to 6 photos of the same space.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the change">
    With **Edit Mode** on **Prompt**, write it in **What would you like to edit?** Say where things go, for example "add a blue sofa on the left wall". To point at a spot instead, switch to **Visual** and click **Add Visual Markers**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-images/interior-edit.webp" alt="Interior Designer with a photo of an empty studio with mirrors and a barre, Add Visual Markers, Add more, mode Edit, Edit Mode Prompt, the instruction to add rolled yoga mats and round bolsters on low wooden shelves and a few sage green floor cushions, and Output Settings 16:9 and 1K" width="450" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the output settings">
    Pick the aspect ratio and the resolution: **1K**, **2K**, or **4K**. 4K is for paid plans.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Generate">
    The result appears on the right in about 20 seconds.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-images/interior-edit-result.webp" alt="The result: the same studio with low wooden shelves of rolled mats and bolsters along the back wall and sage green floor cushions, with Video, Upscale, Edit, and Download buttons, and the Original and the result below to compare" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

Click the thumbnails under the result to compare it with the original. To keep changing the result, describe the next change and click **Generate** again. **Start Fresh** begins again with a new photo.

Each result has:

- **Edit**: opens it in an editing window to change it with a description, like [editing images](/ai-images/edit).
- **Upscale**: opens it in the Magic Upscaler in a new tab. See [Upscale images](/ai-images/upscale).
- **Video**: turns it into a short video, such as a slow walkthrough. See [Generate videos](/videos/generate).
- **Download**, and thumbs up and down.

Your results are also saved in [AI Images](/ai-images/manage).

## Restyle or design from scratch

For **Restyle** and **Generate**, choose the **Room Type**, such as a living room, kitchen, home office, coffee shop, or front yard, and one or more **Themes**: Minimalist, Scandinavian, Industrial, Bohemian, Japanese Zen, Coastal, and many more. **Custom** lets you describe your own. You get one design per theme, so picking three themes makes three designs.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/ai-images/interior-result.webp" alt="A generated minimalist Pilates and barre studio with mirrors, a wooden barre, light oak floors, and floor-to-ceiling windows, labeled Minimalist, with Video, Upscale, Edit, and Download" width="500" />

In **Generate**, you also pick the **Model** and the aspect ratio. Add details in **Additional Prompt (Optional)**, such as colors or furniture you want.

## Tips

- Take the photo in daylight, from a corner, so the whole room is in view.
- In Edit, change one or two things at a time, then build on the result.
- For paint colors, name the color and the finish, such as "soft sage green, matte".

## What it costs

Interior Designer uses [image & video credits](/account/plans-and-credits):

| Mode | Credits per image |
| --- | --- |
| **Edit**, **Restyle**, **Declutter Room**, **Virtual Staging**, **Empty Room**, **Color / Paint Changer** | 7 at 1K or 2K, 12 at 4K |
| **Generate** | Depends on the model: 4 with the default, Nano Banana 2 |
| **360° Panorama** | 3, or 11 with your own photos |

<Note>
  For the photo modes, the **Generate** button currently shows a lower number than what's charged. The table above is what's used.
</Note>

On the Free plan, you can pick one theme at a time, and the photo modes need more credits than the Free plan's 3 a month.
