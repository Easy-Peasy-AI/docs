## Where your images are

- **The Images tab** on AI Images shows your latest results.
- **History** > **Images** has everything you've made, including videos. Search by prompt, filter by date, and on a team, filter by person.
- **History** > **Favorites** collects the images you've marked with the heart.

## Image details

Click an image to see how it was made: the model, the prompt (with a button to copy it), the credits it used, and the date. The same actions are here as buttons, plus **Remove Background** and **Delete**.

## Share an image

- **Share with Team** in the details makes the image visible to everyone on your team. Teams plans only.
- **Share** gives you a public link, a page for the image on easy-peasy.ai, and buttons to post it to LinkedIn, Facebook, X, and Reddit. Images you share publicly can also appear in **Examples** for other people to see. Turn **Share Publicly** off to take the page down.

Images stay private unless you share them.

## Turn an image into a video

Click **Generate Video**, pick a video model and a length, and describe the motion. The video appears on the [AI Videos](/videos/generate) page when it's ready. Video uses image & video credits and is available on paid plans.

## Get credits back for a bad result

If an image came out wrong, click the thumbs-down on it. You get its credits back, up to 10% of your plan's monthly credits in each billing period. Your feedback also helps us improve the models.

## Delete an image

Open the image and click **Delete**. Deleted images can't be restored, and deleting doesn't return the credits they used.

## Download

**Download** saves the image in its original format. On the Free plan, downloading needs bonus credits; see [Plans and credits](/account/plans-and-credits#the-free-plan).
