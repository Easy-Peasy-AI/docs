**AI Photo Studio** makes new photos of a person from a few pictures of them: professional portraits, lifestyle shots, travel photos, or creative looks. You set up a **model** of the person once, then describe any scene with them in it.

Use it for profile pictures, social media, marketing with your team's faces, or fun portraits. Only upload photos of yourself, or of people who agreed to it.

<Note>
  Creating a model and photo packs are for paid plans.
</Note>

## Create a model

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI Photo Studio">
    Click **Images** > **AI Photo Studio** in the top menu, or go to [easy-peasy.ai/ai-images/models](https://easy-peasy.ai/ai-images/models). Click **Start Your AI Photo Shoot Now**, or **Train New Model** if you already have one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload 3 to 10 photos">
    Drag in photos of the same person, PNG or JPEG. Use sharp, well-lit photos where only that person is in view, from different angles and in different settings. Avoid group photos and blurry or heavily filtered pictures.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-images/photo-studio-upload.webp" alt="Upload 3-10 images of the character, with an upload area and three uploaded photos of the same curly-haired woman: a studio portrait, in a white shirt in a yoga studio, and laughing in a park, and a Continue button" width="480" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name the model">
    Click **Continue**, then enter a **Name** and choose the **Gender**, and click **Continue**. The model is ready straight away.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-images/photo-studio-info.webp" alt="Add additional information with Name Maya and Gender Female, and Continue" width="440" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick photo packs, or skip them">
    Next you can choose [photo packs](#photo-packs). To create your own scenes instead, go back to **AI Photo Studio** and click **Run Model**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create photos from a prompt

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/ai-images/photo-studio-models.webp" alt="Your AI models with one model, Maya, created on 9/26/2026, a Run Model button and a menu, and Train New Model" width="560" />
</Frame>

Click **Run Model**. AI Images opens with your model selected under **Model**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe the scene">
    Write the prompt with the word **Model** where the person should be, for example: "Model teaching a morning yoga class on a wooden deck by the sea, smiling, wide shot, photorealistic".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a rendering model">
    **Rendering model** decides which image model draws the photo. **Hyperrealism** gives the most lifelike results.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-images/photo-studio-run.webp" alt="The Image Generator with the prompt Model teaching a morning yoga class on a wooden deck by the sea, the Customize options, Model set to Maya, Custom trained model, Rendering model Hyperrealism, and Generate (3 credits)" width="450" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Generate">
    The photos appear with your other images a few seconds later.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/ai-images/photo-studio-result.webp" alt="A generated photo of the same woman sitting cross-legged on a wooden deck by the sea, leading a group yoga class" width="480" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Rendering model | Credits per image |
| --- | --- |
| **Hyperrealism** (default) | 3 |
| **Nano Banana 2** | 4 |
| **Nano Banana Pro** | 7 |
| **Nano Banana 2 Lite** | 2 |
| **Seedream 5 Pro** | 8 |
| **Seedream 5 Lite** | 2 |
| **Legacy** | 2 |

Everything else in AI Images works as usual: aspect ratio, number of images, styles, and editing the results. See [Generate images](/ai-images/generate).

## Photo packs

Photo packs are ready-made photo shoots: pick a pack, such as Halloween, fitness, or business portraits, and get a whole set of photos of your person in that theme, without writing prompts. Search the packs, or filter them by **Category** and **Gender**.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/ai-images/photo-studio-packs-footer.webp" alt="The photo packs bar: Back, 0 packs selected, Credits: 398 remaining, and Generate Photos" />
</Frame>

Each pack says how many photos it makes, about 30 to 100. Select one or more packs and click **Generate Photos**. The photos appear in AI Images.

Photo packs use 3 image & video credits per photo, so a 32-photo pack uses 96 credits. The bar at the bottom shows how many packs you've selected and the credits you have left.

## Manage your models

**AI Photo Studio** lists your models, including [headshot](/ai-images/headshots) orders. To delete one, click the ⋯ menu next to **Run Model**, then **Delete**.
