---
title: "Upscale images"
description: "Make an image sharper and two or four times larger, add detail, or restore an old photo with the Magic Upscaler."
---

The Magic Upscaler enlarges an image and sharpens it, so it holds up in print or on a big screen. It can also add fresh detail, or clean up an old, faded photo.

</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Magic Upscaler">
    Choose **Images** > **Magic Upscaler** from the menu at the top, and upload a JPG, PNG, or WebP image up to 2,000 pixels wide.

    To upscale an image you made, hover over it and click the sparkles button (**Improve**), or open it and click **Upscale image**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a mode">
    | Mode | Use it for |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Crisp** | A sharper, larger copy that stays true to the original. Start here. |
    | **Creative** | Adding new detail and texture. Sliders for **Creativity**, **HDR**, **Resemblance**, and **Fractality** control how far it can stray from the original. |
    | **Restore old photo** | Old, blurry, or damaged photos. |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the size and upscale">
    Pick a **Scale Factor** of 2 or 4, then click **Upscale**. Drag the slider over the result to compare **Before** and **After**, and click **Download** to save it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The upscaled image is also saved in **History** > **Images**. When you upscale one of your own results, it gets a **Download upscaled image** button next to the original.
