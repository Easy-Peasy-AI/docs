Easy-Peasy.AI brings the AI you use for work into one place. Ask Marky Agent to handle a task, generate images and videos with current models, turn scripts into speech, transcribe recordings, or build an AI agent that answers your customers on your website and messaging apps. Everything runs on one account and one plan.

<Card title="Quickstart" icon="rocket" href="/get-started/quickstart" horizontal>
  Give Marky a task, run a tool, and find your results in about five minutes.
</Card>
