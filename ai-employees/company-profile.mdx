title: "Company profile"
description: "Tell every employee about your business once: what you do, your customers, brand voice, house rules, and key facts. Draft it from your website."

The company profile is what every employee knows about your business. Every employee you hire reads it in every conversation, routine, and group chat, so you don't have to explain your business to each one. They follow your brand voice and house rules, and take names, prices, links, and policies from here instead of guessing.

## Open the company profile

Click **AI Employees** in the sidebar, then **Company profile**. Until you fill it in, the row shows **Set up**.

## Draft it from your website

The fastest start is your website.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your website">
    Under **Start from your website**, enter your website's address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft it">
    Click **Draft from website**. We read your home page and a few pages it links to, such as about, pricing, and help pages, and fill in the profile. It takes up to a minute, and you can keep editing meanwhile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check it and save">
    Read every field, fix anything that's wrong or missing, then click **Save**. Nothing is saved until you do.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Once the profile has content, the same section is called **Fill in from your website**, and it only fills the fields that are still empty. What you've written stays as it is. **House rules** are never drafted: only you write them.

If you added your website during [team onboarding](/ai-employees/hire#hire-a-team), your team already drafted and saved the profile. Check what they learned.

<Note>
  Some websites load their text with JavaScript, so we can't read much of them. If the draft says your website gave us very little text, fill in the rest yourself.
</Note>

## What goes in it

| Field | What to write | Up to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Company name** | Your business's name, such as "Aurela". | 120 characters |
| **Website** | Your website's address. | 300 characters |
| **What the company does** | Products or services, where you work, and what makes you different. | 2,000 characters |
| **Customers** | Who buys from you and what they care about. | 1,000 characters |
| **Brand voice** | How the company sounds: tone, words to use or avoid, the sign-off. | 1,000 characters |
| **House rules** | Things every employee must always or never do, such as "Never promise a delivery date." | 2,000 characters |
| **Key facts** | Prices, plans, opening hours, links, contacts, and policies. | 3,000 characters |

## Update it from a chat

You can also ask any employee in a chat, for example "Add to the company profile that we ship to the whole EU." The employee updates the field and tells you what it changed. Only you can have it changed, while you chat with the employee. Routines and other people can't change it.

The page shows when the profile was last updated, and by whom.

## How it fits with each employee's instructions

The company profile is for what's true for every employee. Each employee's own [instructions](/ai-employees/profile#instructions) are for its role. Where they disagree, the employee's instructions win.

People you [invite to a group chat](/ai-employees/invite-people) and your team in [Slack](/ai-employees/slack) get answers that follow the company profile too.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="The profile changed while I was editing">
    An employee may have updated it while you had the page open. When you save, the page says so. Click **Load latest** to see the newer version (your unsaved changes are dropped), or **Keep mine** to save your version over it.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do employees still need instructions?">
    Yes, for their role: what they do, how they work, and what they ask you first. Put what every employee needs in the company profile, and only role-specific rules in each employee's instructions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
