title: "Knowledge, memory, and skills"
description: "Give an employee documents to answer from, see and edit what it remembers, and choose the skills it uses."
---

Three things make an employee good at your work: **Knowledge** (your documents and pages, which it searches before it answers), **Memory** (what it learns about you and your business as it works), and **Skills** (step-by-step guides for jobs such as research reports). You find all three in the employee's workspace (**Manage** in its direct message).

What every employee should know, such as what your business does and your brand voice, belongs in the [company profile](/ai-employees/company-profile) instead. You write it once, and every employee reads it.

## Knowledge

Knowledge is the material an employee works from: product docs, policies, price lists, FAQs. It searches its knowledge before it answers, so replies are grounded in your sources instead of guesses.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Knowledge">
    In the employee's workspace, click **Knowledge**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your sources">
    Click **Add knowledge**, then upload files, add websites, or paste text. Sources work like an agent's knowledge; see [Knowledge](/agents/knowledge) in the Agents docs for every kind of source.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for training">
    Each source shows its status. When it's ready, the employee can search it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To have an employee answer from the same knowledge as one of your [agents](/agents/overview), pick that agent under **Knowledge base** in the employee's [Profile](/ai-employees/profile#knowledge-base). **Knowledge** then shows which agent it uses, and **Manage sources** opens that agent's knowledge, where you change it for both.

Only you can add knowledge to your employees.

## Memory

An employee remembers what matters between conversations. It writes most of its memory itself, and you can read and edit all of it under **Memory**.

### Notes

Four notes that are in the employee's mind in every conversation:

| Note | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| **Persona notes** | How it refined its role and voice. |
| **About your business** | Facts about your company, products, and customers. |
| **Working memory** | Current projects, logs, and numbers to compare against. |
| **Your preferences** | How you like things done. |

Click into a note to change it, then click **Save**. Each note holds up to 4,000 characters.

### Learned facts

After conversations and routine runs, the employee saves short, dated facts it picked up, such as "Ana at Luma Boutique orders 24 bottles each season". **Profile** facts are always in mind; the rest are recalled when they're recent or relevant, and the employee can search them.

- **To add a fact**, pick its kind (**Profile**, **Log**, or **Note**), type it, and add it.
- **To remove a fact**, click **Forget this fact** next to it.

You can also tell the employee in a chat: "Remember that we never ship on Fridays" or "Forget what I said about the spring discount."

### What doesn't become memory

To keep outside text from changing how an employee behaves, some work never writes to its memory:

- [Routines](/ai-employees/routines) that start from a webhook call or an email. They can read memory but not change it.
- Requests from people you [invited to a group chat](/ai-employees/invite-people), and conversations in [Slack](/ai-employees/slack). There the employee reads its four notes, but not its learned facts, which come from your private conversations.

Marky's [memory](/marky/memory) is separate. Your employees don't read it, and Marky doesn't read theirs.

## Skills

Skills are step-by-step guides an employee follows for specific jobs, such as research reports, data analysis, or documents. They come from the same library as [Marky's skills](/marky/skills), including skills you write yourself.

In the employee's workspace, click **Skills** and choose:

| Option | What the employee uses |
| --- | --- |
| **Every active skill** | Every skill that's turned on in your library, including skills you add later. |
| **Only the skills I pick** | Only the skills you tick: a focused toolbox for its role. **Use the recommended set** ticks the skills suggested for the role. |

Click **Manage the library** to add, write, or turn off skills. A skill turned off in the library isn't used by any employee.
