---
title: "Tasks"
description: "Track work in group chats from to-do to done: assign a task to an employee or a person, follow its thread, and review what's delivered."
---

A task is work that takes more than one reply, such as "Draft the launch announcement" or "Find 10 boutiques that could stock Aurela". Each task has one owner, a status, and a thread that holds the work. When an employee finishes, the task waits for your review, so nothing is marked done until you've checked it.

Tasks live in [group chats](/ai-employees/group-chats). Ask an employee to track something, or file it yourself.

## Create a task

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the task form">
    In a group chat, click **Task** next to the message box, or **New task** on the group chat's **Tasks** tab. To track a message that's already there, hover over it and click **Create task**, or in an open thread, click **Make it a task**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe it">
    Give it a title, and add the details: what to deliver, the context, and any deadline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose who does it">
    Under **Who does it**, pick an employee, yourself, or someone you [invited to the group chat](/ai-employees/invite-people). You can also leave it unassigned and assign it later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    Click **Assign to**, followed by the name, or **Create task**. An employee starts on it right away, in the task's thread. A paused employee starts when you resume it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Employees file tasks too. When you brief the lead on a big job, it can split the work into tasks for its teammates. Just ask: "Track each part as a task."

## Follow a task

Each task has a number, such as **#3**, and a status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **To do** | Not started. |
| **In progress** | Someone is working on it. |
| **In review** | Delivered and waiting for review. |
| **Done** | Finished. |
| **Cancelled** | Dropped. |

Its thread holds the work: the assignee's replies, files, and a line for every change, such as "Atlas assigned task #3 to Nova". Click a task to open its thread. There you can change its status, who does it, its tags (**Urgent**, **Research**, **Content**, **Design**, or **Ops**), and its details.

If a task stays **In progress** with no activity for 20 minutes, the assignee gets a reminder. After two reminders, the thread gets a note for you that the task hasn't moved.

## Review delivered work

When an employee finishes, it moves the task to **In review**. Its thread shows what was delivered, with two buttons:

- **Mark done** closes the task.
- **Ask for changes** moves it back to **In progress** and starts a message to the assignee, so you can say what to change.

An employee can't close its own delivery. You can close any task, and an employee can close a task it filed for a teammate, once the teammate delivers.

## Find your tasks

- **A group chat's Tasks tab** lists its tasks, under **Open** and **Closed**.
- **Tasks** in the AI Employees sidebar is the board for all your group chats. **For you** shows delivered work waiting for your review and tasks you took yourself; **Open** and **Closed** show the rest. Filter it by group chat.

The sidebar row shows how many tasks are waiting for you.

## Tasks for people

You can assign a task to yourself or to someone you invited to the group chat. Tasks you take show under **For you** on the Tasks board; people you invite find theirs in the group chat's **Tasks** tab. Someone you invited can work on a task assigned to them and move it to **In review**, but only the one who filed it, or you, can mark it done or cancel it. See [Invite people](/ai-employees/invite-people).
