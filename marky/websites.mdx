Marky can build a complete website or web app from a description: a landing page for a launch, a portfolio, a booking site, a dashboard, a small game, or an app with sign-in and a database. You see the result in a live preview as it's built, ask for changes in plain language, and publish it when you're happy.

## Build a site

<Steps>
  <Step title="Switch to Build website">
    Start a new chat and click **Build website** under the message box.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/marky/website-mode.webp" alt="The message box in Website mode, a Recommendation to use Claude Opus 5 with Dismiss and Switch model buttons, and Browse templates with the categories Landing page, Dashboard, Portfolio, Corporate, SaaS, Link-in-bio, and E-commerce, and ideas such as Product launch page and Event registration page" />
    </Frame>

    Marky suggests **Claude Opus 5** for building, because it makes fewer mistakes on large projects. Click **Switch model** to use it, or **Dismiss** to stay on your current model. See [Models and words](/marky/models) for what each model costs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the site">
    Say what the site is for, the sections you want, and the look. For example: "A one-page website for Sunrise Yoga, a small yoga studio in Austin: a hero section, our classes, a weekly schedule, pricing, and a contact form."

    For ideas, pick a category under **Browse templates** and click one. To copy the look of a site you like, click **Use reference URL** and paste its address; Marky takes a screenshot and uses it as a visual reference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch Marky build">
    Marky plans the work, finds images, writes the code, and checks the result in its own browser. The workspace opens on the right. In the **Code** tab you can watch each file being written.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/marky/website-code-streaming.webp" alt="Marky writing the site's files in the chat, with Writing file Classes.tsx and Schedule.tsx steps, and the Code tab on the right showing the project's files and the schedule component being written" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview and refine">
    When the site is ready, Marky posts a **Website delivered** card; click **Open** to see it again later. The **Browser** tab shows the live preview. Switch between the desktop and mobile view, refresh it, or open it in a new tab. Then ask for changes in the chat: "Make the hero image brighter", "Add a FAQ section above the contact form", "Use our logo from the attached file."

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/marky/website-preview.webp" alt="The Browser tab of the workspace showing the finished yoga studio site: a navigation bar, a Book a class button, the headline Begin your day the way the sun does, slowly, and with intention, and buttons for the weekly schedule and pricing" width="600" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish** at the top of the workspace. Building and publishing takes a few minutes. When it's done, **Published Successfully!** shows your site's address, which ends in `.easy-peasy.site` and is based on the project's name. Copy it, share it on social media, or click **Visit Your Site**.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/marky/website-published.webp" alt="The Published Successfully! window with the site URL, a Copy button, share buttons for LinkedIn, Facebook, X.com, and Reddit, and a Visit Your Site button" width="500" />
    </Frame>

    You can also ask Marky to publish the site; it asks you to confirm first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Changes you make after publishing show up in the preview right away, but not on your live site. Click **Publish** again, or **Re-publish** in **Settings** > **General**, to put them live.
</Note>

## Websites, web apps, and mobile apps

Marky picks the right setup from your description:

| Project | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Website** | Pages, sections, forms, and images. Most landing pages, portfolios, and company sites. |
| **Web app** | Everything in a website, plus user accounts, a database, and AI features. Ask for it when people need to sign in or save data: "a booking app where customers sign up and reserve a class". |
| **Mobile app** | An app built with Expo. Start it with **More** > **Build app** on a new chat. See [Mobile apps](#mobile-apps). |

## The workspace

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Browser** | The live preview, with **Desktop view** and **Mobile view**, **Refresh**, **Open in new tab**, and **Copy URL**. Mobile apps show **Preview** instead. |
| **Code** | The project's files. Open any file to read it. |
| **Database** | The app's tables and their rows, for web apps with a database. |
| **Storage** | Files your app's visitors upload, such as profile pictures. Copy their links or delete them. |
| **Settings** | Publishing, domains, database access, secrets, and GitHub. See below. |

The **⋯** menu has **Version history**, **Push to GitHub**, and **Download ZIP**. To show or hide the workspace, use the monitor icon at the top of the chat.

### If the preview says the sandbox is paused

Marky's computer pauses when a project isn't used for a while. Click **Resume Sandbox** to wake it up; your files are kept. Published sites aren't affected: they keep running on their own.

## Mobile apps

For a mobile app, the **Preview** tab shows the app in the browser and a QR code. Scan it with the Expo Go app on your phone to try the app on a real device.

When the app is ready, click **Build** at the top of the workspace:

- **Android**: choose **Build APK** for a file you can install on any Android phone for testing, or **Build for Google Play** for an AAB file to upload to the Play Store. A build takes 5 to 15 minutes, then a download button appears. Android builds need a paid plan.
- **iOS**: coming soon.

## Undo changes with version history

Marky saves versions of your project as it works. Open **⋯** > **Version history** to see them, newest first.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/marky/website-version-history.webp" alt="Version history with three versions: the newest marked Live with a Visit button, and two older versions with Rollback buttons, each with a description, date, and thumbnail" />
</Frame>

- The published version is marked **Live**. **Visit** opens it.
- If your newest version isn't published yet, it's marked **Current** and has a **Publish** button.
- **Rollback** returns the project to an older version. You're asked whether to roll back the live site too.

## Settings

Click the gear icon in the workspace.

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/marky/website-settings-general.webp" alt="General Settings for a published site: the project name, the published URL with copy and open buttons, a Re-publish button, and an Always-On Hosting switch with its price" />
</Frame>

| Section | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | The published address, **Re-publish** to put your latest changes live, and **Always-On Hosting**. |
| **Domains** | Your easy-peasy.site address and custom domains. |
| **Database** | The database address for web apps, with **Download as .env**. |
| **Secrets** | API keys and other private settings your app needs, such as a payment provider's key. Click **Add Secret**, then **Save**. They stay on the server and never appear in the site's code. |
| **GitHub** | Connect a GitHub account to keep a copy of the code in a repository. |

### Change the address

Your site gets a free address ending in `easy-peasy.site`. To change the first part, open **Settings** > **Domains**, click **Edit** next to **Easy-Peasy subdomain**, type the new name, and click **Save subdomain**. The new address works right away, and the old one stops working, so update any links you've shared.

  <img src="/images/marky/website-domains.webp" alt="The Domains settings with the Easy-Peasy subdomain being edited to example-yoga-demo, Cancel and Save subdomain buttons, and below them the Custom Domains options Buy a domain and Connect existing domain" />

### Use your own domain

Publish the site first, then open **Settings** > **Domains** > **Custom Domains**:

- **Buy a domain**: search for a name, check the yearly price, and click **Buy**. You pay by card, and the domain is connected for you. Domains are registered for one year.
- **Connect existing domain**: enter a domain you own and click **Add Domain**, then add the DNS record shown at your domain registrar. The status goes from **Pending DNS** to **SSL Provisioning** to **Active**. Click **Check again** to refresh it.

If you connect a main domain such as `example.com`, `www.example.com` works too.

### Always-On Hosting

Published sites go to sleep when nobody visits them, and the first visit afterwards takes a few seconds to load. **Always-On Hosting** keeps the site awake, so every visit is fast. It's a paid add-on for each site, available on paid plans. You'll see the price before you add it in **Settings** > **General**.

## Keep a copy of the code

- **Download ZIP**: **⋯** > **Download ZIP** gives you the whole project.
- **Push to GitHub**: **⋯** > **Push to GitHub** creates a new repository (private or public) or links an existing one, then pushes the code. Use **Sync Changes** later to push new versions.

For safety, Marky never pushes your secrets or `.env` files to GitHub.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is my published site public?">
    Yes. Anyone with the address can visit it. The preview in the **Browser** tab uses a private, hard-to-guess address, but it isn't password-protected either.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens to my site if I cancel my plan?">
    If you built the site on a paid plan and the plan ends, the site's hosting is paused. Renew your plan to bring it back.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I take a site offline?">
    Open **Settings** > **Data Controls** on the dashboard, find the site under **My Websites** or **My Apps**, and delete it. Deleting the chat where you built it also takes the site offline. See [Manage your chats](/marky/manage-chats#delete-a-chat).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I edit the code myself?">
    Yes. Download the ZIP or push it to GitHub and work on it with your own tools. To keep building with Marky, ask for changes in the chat.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
