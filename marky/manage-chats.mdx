---
title: "Manage your chats"
description: "Find old chats, organize them in folders, share a chat with a public link, and delete chats you don't need."
---

All your Marky conversations are in the sidebar under **Your Chats**, with favorites first and then the newest. A small spinner next to a chat means Marky is still working on it.

To collapse the sidebar, click the panel icon at the top. **Back to Home** returns to the dashboard.

## Find a chat

Type in **Search...** in the sidebar. It searches chat titles and the text of your messages.

Your Marky chats also appear in **History** > **Text** on the dashboard, where **Open Chat** takes you back to one.

## Organize chats

Hover over a chat and click **⋯** for these options:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Favorite** | Pins the chat to the top of the list. |
| **Share** | Creates a public link. See [Share a chat](#share-a-chat). |
| **Move to** | Moves the chat into a folder, or back to **Uncategorized**. |
| **Rename** | Changes the chat's title. |
| **Delete** | Deletes the chat for good. See [Delete a chat](#delete-a-chat). |

To make a folder, click the folder icon next to **Your Chats** and type a name. You can also drag chats onto a folder. Deleting a folder moves its chats to **Uncategorized**; the chats aren't deleted.

## Share a chat

1. Click the share icon at the top of the chat, or **Share** in the chat's **⋯** menu.
2. Turn on **Share Publicly**.
3. Copy the link, or share it straight to LinkedIn, Facebook, X, or Reddit.

Anyone with the link can read the conversation, but can't continue it. Turn **Share Publicly** off to take the link down.

On Teams plans, **Share with Team Members** makes the chat visible to your team instead of the public.

## Copy a conversation

To try a different direction without losing the original, open the **⋯** menu under one of Marky's replies and click **Fork chat**. You get a new chat with everything up to that reply, named after the original with "(forked)".

## Delete a chat

Deleting a chat is permanent and can't be undone. It also removes everything Marky made in that chat:

- The chat's messages and the files on Marky's computer.
- Any website or app built in the chat, including the published site, its database, and its settings.
- Scheduled tasks that post to the chat.

Slides and files you downloaded stay on your computer. Images and videos Marky made stay in **History**.

<Warning>
If a chat has a published website, deleting the chat takes the site offline. Download the code first with **⋯** > **Download ZIP** in the workspace if you want to keep it. See [Build websites and apps](/marky/websites).
</Warning>
