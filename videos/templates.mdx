Templates are finished videos you can remake. Each one comes with its prompt, model, length, and shape already set, and many are built around a photo of a person, so you can put yourself in the scene.

## Use a template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the templates">
    Click the **Templates** tab above your results, or the **Templates** button at the top of the page. Pick a category to narrow the list: **Starring You** has templates made for your own photo, and **New** has the latest additions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click a template">
    The template fills in the prompt, the model, the length and shape, and any sample photos it needs. Its name appears at the top of the page, with a button to share the template and **✕** to remove it.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/videos/template-applied.webp" alt="The Giant Dandelion Ride template applied: its name at the top with share and remove buttons, a sample Character photo under References (optional), the filled-in prompt, 15s, 16:9, 480p, Audio, and Generate (90 credits)" width="480" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Put yourself in it">
    If the template uses a sample photo, such as the **Character** above, remove it with **✕**, then click **Add media** and add a photo of yourself or your product. Keep the prompt as it is: it already refers to the photo as **@image1**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Adjust and generate">
    Change anything you like, such as the length or the wording, and click **Generate**. The button shows the price for the template's settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Removing the template with **✕** clears the settings it filled in, except the ones you've changed.

## Templates that open other tools

A few templates open a different tool with everything set up: [Motion Control](/videos/motion-control), [talking videos](/videos/talking-videos), or a workflow in a new tab.

## What templates cost

A template costs what its model costs for its settings, shown on the **Generate** button. Many **Starring You** templates make 15-second videos with Seedance 2.5 Reference, which costs 90 credits at 480p. On the Free plan, only the templates that use Seedance 2.0 Mini work.

## Examples

The **Examples** tab shows videos other people chose to share publicly. **Recreate** copies an example's prompt into the form, so you can make your own version.
