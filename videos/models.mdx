You choose a model under **Model** on the [Video Generator](/videos/generate). This page lists the same models, grouped by family, in the same order as the list.

## How prices work

- **Per second.** Most models charge per second of video, so a 10-second video costs twice as much as a 5-second one. A few older models charge a set price per video.
- **By resolution.** Higher resolutions cost more. The table shows the price per second at each resolution.
- **Sound.** Most models make sound with the video. On some, a silent video costs less, as shown in the table.
- **References.** Reference videos add to the price on some models. See [Use references](/videos/references#what-references-cost).
- **Shown before you start.** The **Generate** button shows the total for your settings. Totals are rounded up to whole credits.

The default model on paid plans is **MiniMax H3**: a 4-second video at 768p costs 12 credits.

## Video models

"Starts from" lists what each family can make a video from. A family's image and reference models appear in the list when you add a start image or references; for example, **Seedance 2.5** becomes **Seedance 2.5 Image** or **Seedance 2.5 Reference**.

## Picking a model

- **To try an idea cheaply**: Seedance 2.0 Mini at 480p, 2 credits per second.
- **For long takes**: Seedance 2.5 and WAN 3.0 make up to 30 seconds in one video.
- **For a person or product that stays the same**: a model that takes reference images, such as Seedance 2.5 Reference.
- **For speed**: MiniMax H3 Max renders in seconds rather than minutes.

Other video tools have their own prices: see [Extend a video](/videos/extend), [Edit a video with AI](/videos/edit), [Upscale a video](/videos/upscale), [Motion Control](/videos/motion-control), and [Talking videos](/videos/talking-videos).
