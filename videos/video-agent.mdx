The Video Agent turns an idea into a complete, edited video. It writes the script, picks or generates visuals for each scene, records a voiceover, adds music and captions, and assembles everything as a project in the [Video Editor](/videos/video-editor), where you can change anything before you export.

Open **Videos** > **Video Agent**. The Video Agent is in beta.

## Make a video

<Steps>
  <Step title="Describe your idea">
    In **Your idea**, say what the video is for, who it's for, the tone, and what should happen. A sentence works; a paragraph works better. For example: "A 30-second Instagram reel for Example Yoga Studio announcing a new sunrise class every weekday at 6:30. Calm, warm, inviting tone. Show the studio at dawn, a small group stretching, and end with: your first class is free."

    **Surprise me** fills in an example idea. **Reference images** lets you add up to 6 photos, such as a real property, product, or place, and the scenes are based on them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the format and length">
    Under **Format & length**, pick **Portrait** (9:16, the default), **Landscape** (16:9), or **Square** (1:1), and a **Target duration** from 10 to 200 seconds. **Auto** lets the agent pick the length, usually 20 to 60 seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the visuals">
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/videos/agent-visuals.webp" alt="The Visuals section: Media type cards Image story (selected), Video clips, Stock videos, and Your own media; Characters (optional) with Create your first character; and Style cards Default (selected), Cinematic, Ghibli Studio, Educational, Pixar, Anime, Watercolor, and Comic Book" />
    </Frame>

    | Media type | What the scenes are made of |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Image story** (default) | An AI image for each scene, with motion, timed to the narration. |
    | **Video clips** | An AI video clip for each scene. Pick the **Video model**; each shows its price per second. Paid plans only. |
    | **Stock videos** | Real stock footage that matches your script. |
    | **Your own media** | Up to 20 of your own photos and videos, 50 MB each, with narration written around them. **Enhance photos** improves them with AI, and **Turn images into videos** animates each photo (paid plans). |

    **Characters (optional)** puts the same person, with the same face, in every scene: pick one of your [custom actors](/videos/talking-videos#your-own-actors) or create one. **Style** sets the look, from **Cinematic** to **Anime** or **Watercolor**; **Show all 27** lists them all.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the voice and music">
    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/videos/agent-audio.webp" alt="The Audio section: Voice with No voiceover or Hope (American) with Change and a play button; Background music tabs Library (selected), Auto, AI Generate, and No music; category chips; and a list of tracks with Tourist selected" />
    </Frame>

    - **Voice**: the narrator. Click **Change** to pick another voice, or choose **No voiceover**.
    - **Background music**: pick a track from the **Library**, let the agent choose (**Auto**), describe a track to create (**AI Generate**, always instrumental), or choose **No music**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the advanced settings (optional)">
    Open **Advanced** to set:

    - **Language** of the narration and captions. **Auto** follows your idea.
    - **Image model** for image scenes. The default is **GPT Image 2.5 Flare Medium**.
    - **Captions** on or off, and the **Caption style**: **Viral** (the default), **Hormozi**, **Wrap**, **Faceless**, **Classic**, or **Minimal**. You can change the style later in the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Generate video">
    The agent starts working and takes you to the **AI Video Editor**, where your project shows **Generating** with a timer. It usually takes 2 to 5 minutes, longer for long videos, and you can leave the page in the meantime.

    <Frame>
      <img src="/images/videos/editor-projects.webp" alt="The AI Video Editor page with a New Project button and, under Recent Projects, the finished 9:16 project A 30-second Instagram reel for Exa..." width="700" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

When it's done, click the project to open it. The video is laid out on tracks for the scenes, on-screen text, music, narration, and captions. Watch it, change what you want, and **Export** it as an MP4. See [Video Editor](/videos/video-editor).

## Credits

There's no total price before you start. Each part is charged as it's made:

| Part | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Scene images | The image model's price per image, 3 credits each with GPT Image 2.5 Flare Medium |
| Video clips | The video model's price per second, shown on each model. Clips without sound cost 20% less. |
| Stock videos and your own photos | Free |
| **Enhance photos** | 4 credits per photo |
| Voiceover | Text-to-speech characters: half the length of the narration |
| Music | **Library** tracks are free. **Auto** and **AI Generate** use 600 text-to-speech characters. |
| Script, captions, and planning | Free |
| Exporting the MP4 in the editor | 2 credits |

On paid plans, the agent checks your balance first and stops with a message if the video would need more credits than you have left. Try a shorter length or a cheaper video model.

If a project fails, it's marked **Generation failed**. Scenes that were already made are still charged; scenes that failed are not.

## On the Free plan

- **Image story**, **Stock videos**, and **Your own media** are available; **Video clips**, characters, and **Turn images into videos** need a paid plan.
- Background music comes from the **Library**.
- You can make 2 projects per billing period, and you need at least 1 credit left.

## Tips

- Say who the video is for and what the viewer should do at the end, such as "book a free class".
- Put names, prices, and times in your idea exactly as they should appear.
- If a scene isn't right, don't start over: open the project and [regenerate that scene](/videos/video-editor#regenerate-a-clip-with-ai).
