Open **AI Videos** in the sidebar, or choose a tool from the **Videos** menu at the top of any tool page. The Video Generator has two halves:

- **On the left**, you describe the video, add a start image or references if you want them, pick a model, and click **Generate**.
- **On the right**, you see your results and ideas to start from.

| Tab on the right | What's in it |
| --- | --- |
| **Videos** | Your latest videos. Everything older is in **History** > **Videos**. |
| **Templates** | Ready-made videos you can remake, many of them with you in the starring role. |
| **Examples** | Videos other people chose to share publicly, for inspiration. |

## The Videos menu

The **Videos** menu at the top of the page opens every video tool:

| Menu item | What it opens |
| --- | --- |
| **Video Agent** | The [Video Agent](/videos/video-agent), which plans, voices, and assembles a whole video. |
| **Video Generator** | The main page, described in [Generate a video](/videos/generate). |
| **Motion Control** | [Motion Control](/videos/motion-control). |
| **Extend Video**, **Edit Video**, **Upscale Video** | The [extend](/videos/extend), [edit](/videos/edit), and [upscale](/videos/upscale) tools. |
| **Talking Videos** | [Talking videos](/videos/talking-videos). |
| **Video Editor** | The [Video Editor](/videos/video-editor). |
| **Seedance 2.5**, **MiniMax H3**, **WAN 3.0**, **Gemini Omni Flash 1.1** | The Video Generator with that model selected. |

## Credits

Videos use image & video credits, the same pool as images. Most models charge per second of video, and the price also depends on the resolution: a 4-second MiniMax H3 video at 768p costs 12 credits. The **Generate** button shows the total before you start. See [Video models and credits](/videos/models) for every model's price, and [Plans and credits](/account/plans-and-credits) for how credits work.

On the Free plan, only a few models are available at short lengths and low resolutions, and extending, editing, upscaling, and Motion Control need a paid plan.
