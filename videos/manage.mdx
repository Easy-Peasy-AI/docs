## Where your videos are

- **The Videos tab** on the Video Generator shows your latest videos. On a team, it also shows videos your teammates shared with the team.
- **History** > **Videos** has every video you've made: generated videos, talking videos, edits, extensions, upscales, and Video Editor exports. Search by prompt or title, filter by date, and on a team, filter by person.

Hover over a video to play it without sound. Click it to open its details.

## The video menu

Each video has a **⋮** menu:

<Frame>
  <img src="/images/videos/video-menu.webp" alt="The menu of a video: Open details, Edit with AI, Upscale video, Upgrade to 2K · MiniMax, Extend video, Regenerate, Download, Share, Good video, Bad video, and Delete" width="440" />
</Frame>

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Open details** | Shows how the video was made. |
| **Edit with AI** | [Edit the video](/videos/edit) by describing a change. |
| **Upscale video** | [Upscale the video](/videos/upscale), up to 4K. |
| **Upgrade to 2K · MiniMax** | Remake a 768p MiniMax H3 video at 2K. See [Upscale a video](/videos/upscale#upgrade-a-minimax-h3-video-to-2k). |
| **Extend video** | [Continue the video](/videos/extend) from its last frame. |
| **Regenerate** | Reopen the tool with the same prompt, model, and settings, so you can make another version. For edits, extensions, and upscales it says **Change these edits**, **Extend again**, or **Upscale again**. |
| **Download** | Save the video as an MP4. |
| **Share** | Get a public link, or share with your team. |
| **Good video**, **Bad video** | Rate the result. **Bad video** also returns its credits. |
| **Delete** | Delete the video. |

## Video details

**Open details** shows the model, the credits the video used, the prompt (with a button to copy it), and the date. The same actions are here as buttons.

  <img src="/images/videos/video-details.webp" alt="The details of a video: model MiniMax H3, 12 credits used, the prompt about a sunlit yoga studio, the creation date, a Share with Team switch, and buttons for Edit with AI, Upscale video, Upgrade to 2K · MiniMax, Extend video, Regenerate, Download video, and Video without sound effects, next to the video" />
</Frame>

Some videos also have **Video without sound effects**, which opens the version before sound was added.

## Share a video

- **Share with Team** in the details shows the video to everyone on your team.
- **Share** opens **Share Video**. Turn on **Share Publicly** to get a public page for the video on easy-peasy.ai, with buttons to post it to LinkedIn, Facebook, X, and Reddit. Turn it off to take the page down. **Share with Team Members** is available on Teams plans.

Videos stay private unless you share them.

## Get credits back for a bad result

If a video came out wrong, open its menu and click **Bad video**. You get its credits back, up to 10% of your plan's monthly credits in each billing period. It works for your own videos from the current billing period. Your feedback also helps us improve the models.

## Delete a video

Open the menu, click **Delete**, and confirm. Deleted videos can't be restored, and deleting doesn't return the credits they used.

## Download

**Download** saves the video as an MP4, with no watermark.
